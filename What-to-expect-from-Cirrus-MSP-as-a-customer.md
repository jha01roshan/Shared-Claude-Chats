# Cirrus MSP: What You Actually Get as a Customer

## Who They Are

Cirrus MSP is a Worcestershire-based managed service provider with over 25 years of experience in technology, operating from Droitwich. They are led by Co-Managing Directors Adam Griffin and Lee Russell, alongside Technical Director Shaun Tolley. Their aim is to act as a one-stop shop for all your technology needs — from the device you work on to the connectivity that links you to the cloud. They hold Microsoft Solutions Partner status, which means they are formally accredited to sell and support the full range of Microsoft 365 business and enterprise plans.

---

## Getting Started: The Onboarding Experience

If you are currently with another IT provider, Cirrus handles the entire transition from start to finish, managing licence and data migration, system configuration, and communication with your existing provider — with the goal of zero downtime for your business. You are not expected to manage the technical handover yourself.

Once onboarded, Cirrus deploys its remote monitoring and management (RMM) tooling across your devices. This runs quietly in the background, giving their engineers visibility of your systems from day one. Your systems are kept up to date and patched as part of the standard service, providing ongoing peace of mind.

---

## Day-to-Day Support: What It Feels Like

Trained engineers based in Worcester are available 8:00am to 5:00pm, Monday to Friday, with extended hours options also available. Issues are attempted to be resolved remotely first, but on-site visit escalations are included as part of the package. You can raise a ticket by phone, email, or through the customer portal at portal.cirrusmsp.co.uk.

Their team explains things in plain English — no jargon, no technical speak, just clear answers you can understand. This matters for businesses where the people dealing with IT issues are not technical specialists.

Pricing is fixed monthly with no hidden fees, making IT budgeting straightforward and predictable. Packages are also flexible: from first to third-line support, consultancy, and projects, they design solutions to fit your business.

## The Full Service Portfolio

### Managed IT Support
The core of what Cirrus delivers is proactive management of your IT environment. Their automated RMM monitors your devices around the clock and applies custom automations to keep everything running smoothly. Daily backup checks are performed, covering not just your servers but also emails, Microsoft Teams chats, SharePoint documents, and OneDrive files — because Microsoft operates under a shared responsibility model, meaning the safety of that data is your responsibility, not Microsoft's, and Cirrus makes sure it is covered.

### Microsoft 365
As a Solutions Partner, Cirrus offers all Microsoft 365 Business and Enterprise subscription plans, and manages the full ecosystem around them. This includes Microsoft Azure (with full migrations, hybrid deployments, and ongoing management), Microsoft Intune for device management and security policies, Microsoft Teams for calling and collaboration, SharePoint for file and document management, and Microsoft Copilot for AI-assisted productivity. They also support education customers with Microsoft 365 Education deployments.

A real-world example of what this looks like in practice: Cirrus successfully transitioned a specialist SEN college from legacy on-premises infrastructure to a fully cloud-based environment using Microsoft 365, enabling a secure and collaborative modern workspace. The college described the migration as smooth, seamless, and stress-free.

### Business Telephony and VoIP
Cirrus offers VoIP solutions through Microsoft Teams (using Direct Routing or Operator Connect) and through Webex, an enterprise-grade platform that integrates directly with Teams — allowing calls to be made and received within the Teams interface while using Webex for the underlying telephony infrastructure.

This is increasingly important: in 2027, the legacy PSTN infrastructure in the UK will be switched off permanently. Any business that wants to continue using telephone services must move to a full fibre internet connection and an internet-compatible device. Cirrus handles the full transition — auditing your existing system, planning and installing the new one, and training your team to use it. VoIP also brings practical benefits including lower call costs, the ability to make and receive calls from anywhere, a mobile app so staff are reachable without giving out personal numbers, and features like call queuing, voicemail to email, video conferencing, and advanced reporting.

### Connectivity
Cirrus manages business internet connectivity, ensuring the underlying network that everything else runs on is reliable, properly specified, and fit for cloud-first working.

### Cyber Security
This is a substantial part of the Cirrus offer and covers multiple layers:

**Endpoint and network protection** — using next-generation firewalls, antivirus and malware protection (via BitDefender), DNS filtering, and email security solutions, in partnership with specialists including Huntress and Fortinet.

**Vulnerability scanning** — using Qualys through the CyberSmart platform, Cirrus runs continuous assessments of your IT environment to detect security gaps, misconfigurations, and outdated software. Scans cover your network, servers, endpoints, and cloud infrastructure without disrupting daily operations. The service produces prioritised, actionable reports so you know which risks to address first.

**Staff awareness training** — including simulated phishing attacks and automated training reminders. This can be fully managed by Cirrus or self-administered. This matters because human error remains the most common entry point for cyber attacks.

**Cyber Essentials certification support** — helping businesses achieve the UK government-backed Cyber Essentials standard.

**Managed SOC (Security Operations Centre)** — for businesses wanting 24/7 threat detection and response beyond standard monitoring.

**Disaster recovery and business continuity** — including recovery strategies to restore systems and data quickly after a cyber incident or outage, and continuity planning to keep key operations running with minimal disruption.

## Visibility and Control: What the Technology Gives You

A key benefit of working with Cirrus is that you are not simply handing over your IT and hoping for the best. Several layers of tooling give you — and them — genuine visibility:

The **NinjaOne RMM platform** monitors all devices 24/7, meaning issues are often detected and resolved before you are even aware of them. The **HaloPSA ticketing system** gives you a structured, trackable view of every support request and its status. The **CyberSmart / Qualys vulnerability platform** produces real-time insight into your security posture, with clear prioritisation rather than a list of technical issues you cannot interpret. The **Dropsuite backup platform** handles verified, external backups of your Microsoft 365 data with daily checks. For businesses requiring compliance reporting — whether for insurance, auditing, or regulation — these tools create a documented, auditable trail of what is happening across your IT environment.

The result is that you move from a reactive relationship with IT (fixing things when they break) to a position where your technology is continuously managed, measured, and improved.

## Who Cirrus MSP Is Best Suited For

Cirrus is a strong fit for UK businesses — particularly those in the Midlands and surrounding regions — that meet one or more of the following:

**You have outgrown ad hoc IT support.** If you are currently relying on a break-fix arrangement or a sole trader IT contact, and your reliance on technology has grown, Cirrus offers the structure and accountability that comes with a proper managed service.

**You want one supplier, not several.** Many businesses juggle separate providers for IT support, telephony, connectivity, and Microsoft licences. Cirrus consolidates all of this under a single contract, single point of contact, and single monthly bill.

**You are concerned about cyber security but don't know where to start.** Cirrus offers a layered approach from endpoint protection through to staff training and vulnerability scanning — practical and proportionate rather than over-engineered.

**You are preparing for the 2027 PSTN switch-off.** If your business still runs on a traditional phone system, now is the time to plan the move to VoIP, and Cirrus manages that transition end to end.

**You are in sectors like accountancy, recruitment, education, manufacturing, agriculture, or the charity sector** — all of which Cirrus identifies as areas they actively serve.

**You want a local, relationship-based provider.** Their Worcestershire-based technicians can easily access customer sites throughout the Midlands, ensuring prompt and efficient service. For businesses that value being able to speak to and meet the people looking after their IT, this matters.
