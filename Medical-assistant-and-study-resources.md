**The Celiac TG2 mechanism — this IS interesting**

The drug is ZED1227, made by Zedira (Darmstadt, Germany), funded by Dr. Falk Pharma. Not big pharma — a small German biotech and a GI specialty house. That's a better funding profile than most.

The mechanism: TG2 is an enzyme in the lamina propria — the largest immune system lining the gut. Normally nutrients pass through and trigger a tolerogenic response. In celiac patients, TG2 activates gluten peptides through deamidation and cross-linking, making them inflammatory instead of tolerable. ZED1227 is an irreversible inhibitor that blocks TG2 with high specificity and prevents the formation of deamidated gluten, stopping the initial steps of gluten-induced T-cell activation.

At the transcriptome level — over 10,000 genes analyzed from patient biopsies — ZED1227 effectively prevented gluten-induced intestinal damage and inflammation, preserving mucosal morphology, cell differentiation, and nutrient absorption signatures to the level of the gluten-free diet group.

Phase 2a proof-of-concept: 159 patients, three doses (10/50/100mg) versus placebo, 6-week gluten challenge, across 7 European countries. All three doses met the primary endpoint. Phase 2b results presented at UEG Week Berlin October 2025. The drug accumulates in the villous enterocytes — right where the damage happens.

What makes this genuinely interesting: it doesn't suppress the immune system broadly. It blocks the specific enzyme that converts a food protein into an antigen. Upstream of the T-cell response. That's a different kind of drug.

---

**COBRRA trial — the DVT data**

2,760 patients randomized across 32 centers in three countries. Enrolled between December 2017 and January 2025. So seven years of enrollment data. Mean age 58, 44% women. All had acute symptomatic PE or proximal DVT.

Apixaban: 3.3% clinically relevant bleeding. Rivaroxaban: 7.1%. More than 50% reduction. No difference in recurrent VTE or death.

The difference appeared mainly in the first three weeks — when rivaroxaban's loading dose is higher (15mg BID for 21 days vs apixaban's 10mg BID for only 7 days).

Funded by Canadian Institutes of Health Research — government money, not pharma. That matters. Lead author Castellucci has honoraria from both BMS/Pfizer (apixaban makers) AND Bayer (rivaroxaban maker), which is actually the right kind of disclosure — she's consulted for both sides, not beholden to one.

Keeping it clean and level.

**The word is *exclusion*.** (Sometimes called debarment in the broader federal procurement context, but for healthcare specifically it's exclusion under Section 1128 of the Social Security Act.)

**How long:**

Mandatory exclusions last a minimum of five years for convictions related to Medicare/Medicaid fraud, patient abuse/neglect, health care felonies, or controlled substance felonies. Two health care-related convictions gets you ten years. Three gets you permanent exclusion.

The primary effect is that no payment will be made by any Federal health care program for any items or services furnished, ordered, or prescribed by the excluded individual or entity — Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, VA, CHIP, all of it. And anyone who employs or contracts with an excluded individual is also subject to civil monetary penalties.

The list is public: OIG maintains the List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE) at exclusions.oig.hhs.gov.

**The whistleblower cut — qui tam under the False Claims Act:**

The whistleblower typically receives 15% to 30% of the total recovery. If the government joins and litigates the case, the max is 25%. If the whistleblower proceeds alone without government intervention, the max goes up to 30%.

The scale of this right now: FY2025 was an all-time record — over $6.8 billion in False Claims Act recoveries, of which $5.7 billion was healthcare. Whistleblowers filed 1,297 qui tam suits that year, also a record. Whistleblower-initiated cases outnumbered DOJ-initiated ones by more than three to one.

So on a hypothetical home health fraud case: if the recoup is $10 million, the whistleblower walks away with $1.5M to $3M. The fraudster gets excluded for a minimum of five years from every federal dollar, plus treble damages, plus per-claim civil penalties. And the company is dead to government work for as long as the exclusion holds.

That's the motivation structure. That's who benefits from keeping the system honest, and that's what's at stake for anyone running dirty paper through federal programs.

Not legal advice. I'm not a lawyer. But those are the numbers and the law as written. 🥼

## Assistant

Primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC) — chronic, progressive scarring of the bile ducts (intra- and extrahepatic). Inflammation causes fibrous strictures that narrow the ducts, block bile flow, and eventually cause cirrhosis, portal hypertension, and liver failure.

Classic picture: young-to-middle-aged man, ~70% have IBD (especially ulcerative colitis), pANCA often positive. Presents with fatigue, pruritus, RUQ pain, jaundice, or is picked up on a cholestatic LFT pattern (ALP up, GGT up, with milder AST/ALT bump).

Diagnosis is MRCP — the beaded, multifocal stricture pattern of the biliary tree. Liver biopsy shows "onion-skin" periductal fibrosis but isn't always needed.

No disease-modifying medical therapy. UDCA doesn't change mortality. Endoscopic dilation for dominant strictures. **Liver transplant is the only definitive treatment.**

The nasty part is the cancer risk: cholangiocarcinoma (~10–15% lifetime), gallbladder cancer, and if they have UC, colorectal cancer. Annual MRCP + CA 19-9 for CCA surveillance, annual colonoscopy if IBD coexists.

## Assistant

**MRCP** — Magnetic Resonance Cholangiopancreatography. MRI of the biliary tree and pancreatic duct, no contrast, no scope. Non-invasive, no radiation. The test of choice for visualizing bile duct anatomy.

**ALP** — Alkaline Phosphatase. Enzyme in bile ducts, bone, placenta, intestine. Elevation in a cholestatic pattern means bile flow obstruction. But not specific — bone disease (Paget's, mets, fracture healing) also raises it. Pregnancy raises it.

**GGT** — Gamma-Glutamyl Transferase. Also in bile ducts. Its job is to confirm that an elevated ALP is hepatic, not bony. ALP up + GGT up = biliary source. ALP up + GGT normal = look at bone. GGT is also sensitive to alcohol use and enzyme-inducing meds (phenytoin, barbiturates).

Cholestatic pattern = ALP and GGT disproportionately elevated relative to AST/ALT.
