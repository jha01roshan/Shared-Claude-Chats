## **Square Setup Option: 245/45 R20 on all four wheels**

**Advantages:**
- Cheaper (buy 4 of one size vs. staggered)
- Tire rotation possible (extends life 20-30%)
- Easier to find/replace individual tires
- Can carry one spare size
- Simpler for dealers

**Disadvantages:**
- Loses some rear grip/handling characteristics BMW engineered in
- Slightly affects handling balance (less rear mechanical grip)
- May void some BMW handling warranties (check with dealer)
- Rear wheels will have narrower tires than designed for
