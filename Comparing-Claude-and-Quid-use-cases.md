Here's a comparison of when to use **Quid** vs. **Claude**:

---

**Real-time social listening and brand monitoring:** Use Quid when you need to track live conversations happening right now across social media, news, blogs, and forums at scale. Quid tracks real-time conversations across social media, blogs, forums, and news sites, offering sentiment analysis and trend detection with 27 months of historical data across 200+ countries. Claude cannot ingest or monitor live social data streams — it has a knowledge cutoff and no persistent connection to the open web.

**Trend forecasting and predictive market intelligence:** Use Quid when you need to predict whether a trend has staying power before committing resources. Quid Predict uses a proprietary model to filter data points, pinpointing what is likely to become important to a brand versus a fad, running a continuous scan to tell users what's important. Claude cannot run continuous scans or apply proprietary predictive models trained on your specific market category.

**Competitive intelligence at enterprise scale:** Use Quid when you need structured, ongoing benchmarking against competitors using aggregated data across millions of sources. Tools like Quid Discover, Monitor, Compete, and Connect allow users to extract meaning from data through clustering, benchmarking, real-time tracking, and relational queries. Claude cannot pull live competitor data, aggregate it across sources, or automatically update dashboards as the market shifts.

**Consumer and market insights tied to your own proprietary data:** Use Quid when you need to blend your internal customer data with external signals for a unified view. Quid ingests over 300 million documents per day and also integrates client-side data, allowing a blended analysis that is both expansive and hyper-relevant. Claude cannot securely ingest and persistently maintain your proprietary datasets alongside live external data at petabyte scale.

**Large-scale text clustering and pattern recognition across massive datasets:** Use Quid when you need to automatically cluster and visualize themes across millions of data points. Quid's AI architecture supports tasks such as entity extraction, topic clustering, summarization, and semantic search across trillions of data points from social, search, market, and proprietary customer sources. Claude can analyze text you provide directly, but cannot autonomously process and cluster millions of documents from live external sources on its own.

---

In short, Quid is a purpose-built market intelligence platform with live data infrastructure. Claude is a general-purpose AI best suited for reasoning, writing, analysis, and tasks where *you* supply the data.

**Open-ended reasoning and synthesis:** Use Claude when you need to think through complex, ambiguous problems that don't have a clear data answer. Quid cannot engage in back-and-forth dialogue to pressure-test assumptions, reframe a problem, or reason through strategy with nuance.

**Writing and content creation:** Use Claude when you need to draft, edit, or refine any form of written content — reports, emails, presentations, ad copy, or creative work. Quid cannot generate original prose, adapt tone for different audiences, or act as a writing collaborator.

**Coding and technical tasks:** Use Claude when you need to write, debug, or explain code across dozens of languages and frameworks. Quid cannot help you build a script, troubleshoot an API integration, or walk you through a technical implementation.

**Summarizing and analyzing documents you already have:** Use Claude when you need to make sense of a report, contract, research paper, or dataset that's in front of you. Quid cannot accept an arbitrary file you upload and reason over its specific contents in a conversational way.

**Custom research and ad hoc Q&A:** Use Claude when you need a knowledgeable generalist to answer questions, explain concepts, or explore a topic across domains — from market strategy to behavioral psychology to legal frameworks. Quid cannot answer open-ended questions outside the scope of its social and market data infrastructure.

**Workflow automation and task execution:** Use Claude when you need to automate multi-step tasks — summarizing findings, formatting outputs, generating variations, or acting as an agent within a broader workflow. Quid cannot be prompted to take generative action; it surfaces insights but doesn't execute downstream tasks on your behalf.

---

The core distinction: Quid tells you *what the market is doing* through structured data. Claude helps you *decide what to do about it* — and execute.
