## Assistant

Here are the most important iPhone security settings for seniors:

## Identity & Access

**Face ID / Touch ID + Strong Passcode**
Enable biometric login and set a 6-digit (or alphanumeric) passcode. Go to *Settings → Face ID & Passcode*. Avoid simple codes like "123456."

**Two-Factor Authentication**
Turn on 2FA for their Apple ID at *Settings → [Name] → Sign-In & Security → Two-Factor Authentication*. This prevents account takeover even if a password is stolen.

---

## Privacy Controls

**Location Services**
Review which apps can see location at *Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services*. Set most apps to "While Using" or "Never" — only Maps/Find My need "Always."

**App Permissions Audit**
Check *Settings → Privacy & Security* and review Camera, Microphone, Contacts, and Photos access. Revoke anything that seems unnecessary.

---

## Scam & Fraud Protection

**Filter Unknown Callers**
*Settings → Phone → Silence Unknown Callers* — sends unrecognized numbers straight to voicemail, cutting off most robocalls and phone scams.

**Filter Unknown Messages**
*Settings → Messages → Filter Unknown Senders* — moves messages from unknown numbers into a separate tab, reducing smishing (SMS phishing) exposure.

**Mail Privacy Protection**
*Settings → Apps → Mail → Privacy Protection → Protect Mail Activity* — stops email senders from knowing when an email is opened or tracking location.

---

## Device Loss & Theft

**Find My iPhone**
Enable at *Settings → [Name] → Find My → Find My iPhone*. Also turn on **Send Last Location** so the device reports its location before the battery dies.

**Auto-Erase After Failed Attempts** *(optional, with caution)*
*Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Erase Data* — wipes the phone after 10 wrong passcode attempts. Only recommended if backups are reliable.

---

## Software & Updates

**Automatic Updates**
*Settings → General → Software Update → Automatic Updates* — turn on all toggles. Security patches are critical and seniors shouldn't have to remember to update manually.

---

## Backup & Recovery

**iCloud Backup**
*Settings → [Name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup → Back Up Now* (and ensure it's set to automatic). A recent backup is the safety net for everything else.

**Recovery Contact**
*Settings → [Name] → Sign-In & Security → Account Recovery → Add Recovery Contact* — designate a trusted family member who can help regain access if locked out.

---

## Bonus: Screen Time for Oversight

If a family member is helping manage the phone, *Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions* can block app installs, in-app purchases, and explicit content — reducing the risk of accidental charges or exposure to scams.

---

**Top 3 to do first:**
1. Strong passcode + Face ID
2. Silence Unknown Callers
3. Automatic Updates

These three alone block the majority of common threats seniors face.
