**Humanoid Atlas** (humanoids.fyi) is a comprehensive, open-source database and interactive map of the humanoid robotics industry. Here's what it covers:

**What it is**
It's a tool for comparing OEMs (robot manufacturers), hardware supply chains, AI software stacks, and human-robot interaction design across the humanoid robot industry. It was created by Julian Saks and is open source on GitHub.

**Key sections:**

- **Robot OEMs (29+)** — Tracks major players like Tesla (Optimus), Figure, Boston Dynamics (Atlas), Unitree, Agility Robotics, Sanctuary AI, and many more.

- **Hardware Supply Chain (41+ suppliers)** — Maps out 10 component categories including motors, reducers, compute chips (like NVIDIA), batteries, sensors, actuators, hands, bearings, and PCBs — noting things like planetary roller screws as a key supply chain bottleneck.

- **AI Models** — Tracks three types:
  - **VLA (Vision-Language-Action) models** — 19 models that unify perception, language, and robot control, including offerings from NVIDIA, Physical Intelligence, Google DeepMind, and others.
  - **Reward Models** — 10 models that score robot task performance for reinforcement learning training.
  - **World Models** — 19 models that simulate how the world evolves in response to robot actions, including ones from NVIDIA, Meta, and DeepMind.

- **Visualization Tools** — 10 tools for debugging and inspecting robot data, like Foxglove, Rerun, and RViz2.

- **Display & Head Designs** — Tracks how 17 humanoid robots communicate with humans, categorizing them into 5 face types: OLED screens, status screens, LED indicators, no display, and concealed.

**In short**, it's essentially a living industry map for anyone wanting to understand the full stack of humanoid robotics — from physical hardware suppliers all the way up to the AI models powering these robots. It's useful for researchers, investors, engineers, or anyone curious about the competitive landscape.
