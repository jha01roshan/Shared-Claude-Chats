The timeline covers 50 decades across seven major eras. A few throughlines worth noting:

**Transitions are slow, then sudden.** Sail and horse dominated for nearly two centuries before canals and steam disrupted them in barely 50 years. Rail then reigned for roughly 70 years before the automobile overtook it. Each new mode coexists with the old for decades before becoming dominant.

**The 20th century compressed everything.** In 1900, the horse was still king. By 1970, humans had walked on the Moon. No other century comes close to that rate of modal change.

**Electrification is the current hinge.** Just as steam unified rail, ships, and factories in the 1800s, electrification is now unifying cars, buses, trucks, bikes, scooters, and potentially aviation under one energy paradigm.

The decades from 2030 onward shift from documented history to informed projections (and beyond 2060, informed speculation), so take those entries as plausible trajectories rather than forecasts. You can filter by era using the buttons at the top to focus on any period that interests you.
