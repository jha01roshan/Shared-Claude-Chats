There isn't really an official name for it, but the phenomenon you're thinking of is usually called **"neuralese."** That's the informal term researchers use for the dense, private shorthand Claude Fable 5 developed for its internal reasoning — a mix of jargon, Morse-style symbols, and emojis that it uses internally before switching back to English with humans.

Anthropic hasn't given it a formal name. The Fable 5 system card just describes the reasoning as denser and harder to interpret than prior models, with more jargon and difficult language, and the prompting guide warns it can produce "dense arrow-chain shorthand" and references to thinking the user never saw. Commentators picked up "neuralese" to describe it — for example, one user noted that Fable invents codenames while reasoning and then fails to realize they're meaningless to the reader, describing it as "its neuralese blending into its output".

So: "neuralese" is the widely used nickname, but it's a community term for the behavior rather than a language Anthropic officially named.
