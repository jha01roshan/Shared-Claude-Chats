## QA Assessment Summary — WP W-02 Inventory Accuracy

**Overall verdict: FAIL.** The working paper is fundamentally incomplete and the conclusion is not supportable by the evidence gathered. Here is the headline diagnosis:

**The core problem is a logical chain that breaks completely at authorization.** The RCM W-02 control requires that IMS adjustments be approved by the *Warehouse Manager* prior to posting. The auditor verified only that a *Shift Lead* signed the count sheet — and then concluded the control is "Operating Effectively." These are different roles with different authority levels, and the distinction is the entire point of the segregation of duties control.

**Three of five ToE requirements were never tested:**

1. Blind counting (that counters could not see system balances) — never examined.
2. Dual-signature on adjustment documents — only one signatory recorded per sample.
3. Warehouse Manager pre-approval in the IMS — Shift Lead signatures accepted instead.

**The 88-unit variance in S-03 is the starkest red flag.** A material quantity discrepancy in Grade A durian stock with zero root cause investigation, zero investigation documentation, and a passing conclusion is the most significant professional judgment failure in the paper.

**To improve this WP, the auditor must:** re-inspect Count Sheets #102, #205, and #309 against all five ToE criteria; obtain IMS workflow records proving Warehouse Manager approval; document root cause analysis for all three variances; and revise the conclusion to "Testing Incomplete" until sufficient evidence is gathered. The current draft should not proceed to reviewer sign-off.
