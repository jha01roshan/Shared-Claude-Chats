# Grapeseed Oil Market Analysis

**Market Size & Growth**
The global grapeseed oil market was valued at USD 692.60 million in 2025 and is projected to reach USD 769.20 million in 2026, growing further to USD 1,780.32 million by 2034, reflecting a CAGR of 11.06% during the 2026–2034 forecast period. Growth is driven by rising demand for plant-based oils with functional and nutritional benefits across food, cosmetics, and pharmaceutical sectors. As a by-product of wine production, grapeseed oil is gaining traction for its antioxidant content, vitamin E, and polyunsaturated fats, along with its light texture and high smoke point.

**Market Dynamics**
The primary driver is rising demand for natural, heart-healthy edible oils, supported by growing use in functional and fortified foods and the foodservice sector's preference for neutral-flavored, heat-tolerant oils. A key restraint is the limited availability of raw material, since supply depends on seasonal grape production and wine output, pushing up procurement and processing costs. A major opportunity lies in expanding cosmetics and personal care applications, where grapeseed oil's non-comedogenic and anti-aging properties suit premium skincare, haircare, and vegan beauty lines. The main challenge is price competition from cheaper alternatives like sunflower, palm, and canola oil, which dominate cost-sensitive markets.

**Segmentation**
By nature, conventional oil leads with 66% share due to affordability and scale, while organic holds 34%, driven by clean-label and sustainability trends. By application, the food industry dominates with 38% share (cooking oils, salad dressings, bakery), followed by cosmetics and personal care (29%), healthcare and supplements (18%), and other industrial uses such as lubricants and bio-based products (15%).

**Regional Outlook**
North America leads with 31% share, powered by the U.S. wine industry and strong demand for premium, clean-label products. Europe follows closely at 30%, with France, Italy, and Spain's wine industries supporting supply, and Germany (9%) and the UK (7%) as key national markets emphasizing organic and premium segments. Asia-Pacific holds 27%, led by China (11%) and Japan (6%), driven by changing dietary habits and rising demand in cosmetics; India and South Korea are also growing contributors. The Rest of World, including Latin America, the Middle East, and Africa, accounts for 12%, with gradual adoption tied to urbanization and rising health awareness.

**Competitive Landscape**
Key players include BORGES AGRICULTURAL & INDUSTRIAL EDIBLE OILS, S.A.U. (19% market share) and Tampieri (15% market share), along with Mediacovrac, Gustav Heess Oleochemische Erzeugnisse GmbH, Salute Santé, Alvinesa Natural Ingredients, Henan Province Kunhua Biological Technology Co., Ltd., VedaOils, and Costa d'Oro. Recent developments (2023–2025) include expansion of organic product lines, launch of cold-pressed premium-grade oils, cosmetic-industry partnerships, increased investment in extraction facilities, and new packaging innovations to extend shelf life.

**Outlook**
The market's trajectory reflects a shift toward organic, cold-pressed products and expanding cosmetic applications, positioning grapeseed oil as a premium alternative within the broader vegetable oils category through 2034.
