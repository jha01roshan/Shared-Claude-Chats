Here's a summary of Triangle Direct Media's (TDM) offerings based on their site:

**Company overview**
TDM is a Cary, North Carolina-based digital marketing agency that has helped businesses grow through expert SEO, paid advertising, and now AI search optimization for over 20 years, having been established in 2006.

**Core services**
- **SEO & AI Visibility (AEO):** Positioned around the shift toward AI-driven search — the company frames Google's AI Overviews as the biggest change to search since mobile, with an "AI-Driven Impact Marketing (AIM)" program already delivering results for eCommerce and service businesses. They pitch this as helping brands get recommended in tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
- **Performance Marketing / PPC Management:** Paid campaign management across Google, Microsoft, and Meta, with a stated benchmark of measurable return on every dollar spent. They also offer conversion analysis and a free "wasted ad spend" audit.
- **Content Strategy:** Content/blog campaigns built to target what buyers are actually searching for rather than filling a content calendar.
- **Link Building:** Domain-authority-focused backlink campaigns, emphasizing quality over quantity — the company states one quality backlink from a relevant industry publication outweighs many from low-value directories.
- **Ecommerce Storefront Optimization & Web Design:** Site and conversion optimization for Shopify, BigCommerce, and MIVA stores.

**Industries served**
Their materials describe work across businesses of all sizes and in almost every business space, with a particular emphasis on both
