# Vitamin K Market Analysis

## Market Overview

The global vitamin K market was valued at USD 1.18 billion in 2025 and is projected to grow from USD 1.29 billion in 2026 to USD 2.75 billion by 2034, exhibiting a CAGR of 9.90% during the forecast period.

Vitamin K occurs mainly in two forms, K1 and K2, and plays a vital role in blood clotting and bone health maintenance. Rising consumer awareness of preventive healthcare is driving demand for dietary supplements that address nutritional gaps, while the vitamin's established cardiovascular and bone-health benefits continue to attract interest. Growth in chronic conditions such as osteoporosis, blood clotting disorders, and diabetes is a key driver, since vitamin K supports management of these conditions.

## Market Drivers & Opportunities

Fortification of functional foods and beverages presents a notable opportunity for market expansion. Additionally, infant nutrition is emerging as a significant growth avenue, with vitamin K supplementation used to prevent vitamin K deficiency bleeding (VKDB) in newborns; health authorities recommend prophylaxis within an hour of birth to prevent severe bleeding in infants.

**By Type:** The K2 segment is expected to hold the largest market share, driven by its broader perceived health benefits including cardiovascular support, bone health, and anti-inflammatory properties, aided by improved fermentation-based production (MK-7). The K1 segment is set for strong growth too, propelled by rising food fortification in cereals and infant formulas.

**By Application:** Osteoporosis dominates the application segment, reflecting its status as a major public health concern, particularly among postmenopausal women and the elderly. VKCFD is expected to see high growth due to rising diagnosis rates and limited existing treatment options. Dermal applications are also gaining traction, as topical vitamin K creams are used for bruising, scarring, and skin inflammation.

**By Route of Administration:** Oral formulations lead the market owing to convenience, affordability, and accessibility compared to parenteral or topical routes. Topical applications are projected to grow significantly as consumers seek localized, easy-to-use treatment options.

**By Distribution Channel:** Pharmacy stores hold the largest share, supported by product variety and professional guidance from pharmacists. Online stores are the fastest-growing channel, offering convenience and broad product access.

## Regional Analysis

North America leads the global market, underpinned by a mature dietary supplements industry and high healthcare expenditure supporting preventive care adoption. Europe is expected to see significant growth, with Germany, France, and the UK driving demand through rising health awareness, increasing disposable incomes, and an aging population.

## Competitive Landscape

Key players in the vitamin K market include Kappa Bioscience, NattoPharma, Gnosis S.p.A., Koninklijke DSM N.V., Viridis BioPharma, Frutarom, DuPont Nutrition & Health, Synergia Life Sciences, and Mankind Pharma.

## Recent Developments

- **April 2023:** Petrovax Pharm launched SunDevit in Russia, a combination product featuring vitamins D3 and K2.
