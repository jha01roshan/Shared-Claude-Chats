### How Loss Estimates Are Calculated: Three Methods

**Method 1: Realized + Unrealized P&L per wallet (blockchain forensics)**
This is the Chainalysis/NYT approach. Every $TRUMP transaction is public on the Solana blockchain. You reconstruct each wallet's cost basis (weighted average purchase price × tokens held), compare to either sale price (realized) or current price (unrealized), and sum across wallets. For the $TRUMP memecoin specifically, losses were calculated by analyzing the blockchain itself, since the project doesn't provide revenue disclosures.

**Method 2: Market cap destruction**
Simple: peak market cap (~$27 billion at launch) minus current market cap (~$393 million as of July 2026) = ~$26.6 billion destroyed. The current market capitalization sits at approximately $393 million, down from $27 billion at launch — a fall of over 98%. But this overstates investor losses because 80% of supply was never in public hands.

**Method 3: Circulating supply × price decline**
Only 20% of 1 billion tokens (200 million) entered public circulation. If the average public buyer paid, say, $20 (between the ~$0.18 launch flash and $74 peak), that's ~$4 billion deployed by the public. At current ~$1.66, that 200 million coins is worth ~$332 million — implying ~$3.7 billion in losses from the circulating supply alone. This aligns reasonably with the $4.5 billion NBC figure.

### Methodological Caveats and Disputes

1. **Unrealized vs. realized losses**: The Reuters $2.3 billion figure includes paper losses on unsold positions. A spokesperson for World Liberty disputed the methods Reuters used to calculate the losses retail investors have seen. If only realized losses (tokens actually sold at a loss) are counted, the number would be lower — though not trivially so.

2. **Who counts as a "victim"**: Wallets ≠ people. One person may hold multiple wallets; some wallets may be bots or arbitrageurs. Chainalysis estimated 2 million wallets, NPR cited 750,000 accounts. A data firm estimated more than 750,000 investment accounts are holding meme coins that are now effectively worthless.

3. **Divergent totals**: Different sources report $2 billion (NYT/Chainalysis, first 19 days), $2.3 billion (Reuters, through April 2026), and $4.5 billion (unnamed blockchain firm, through July 2026). The divergence reflects (a) time period, (b) whether WLFI tokens and related equities are included, and (c) methodology for unrealized losses.

4. **The "not an investment" disclaimer**: This is legally significant. If tokens are not securities, standard fraud remedies may not apply, limiting victims' legal recourse.
