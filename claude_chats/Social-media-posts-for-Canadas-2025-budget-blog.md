This divergence is the story.

---

**Tweet 5**
Three mechanisms to strengthen industrial carbon pricing:

1. Post-2030 trajectory explicitly targeting net-zero by 2050
2. Harmonized provincial carbon credit markets
3. Carbon contracts for difference via Canada Growth Fund

Long-term price certainty for multi-decade investment decisions.

---

**Tweet 6**
The oil & gas emissions cap gets an explicit off-ramp.

It's now conditional on carbon market effectiveness and CCS deployment at scale. If those work, "the cap would no longer be required."

A bet on technology and markets over mandatory limits.

---

**Tweet 7**
Regulatory flexibility replaces rigid mandates across the board:

→ Clean Electricity Regs: more provincial flexibility
→ EV mandate: 2026 target removed, focus shifts to infrastructure
→ O&G cap: conditional, not absolute

Collaborative federalism over coercive.

---

**Tweet 8**
Investment tax credits become the primary tool:

→ Clean Tech ITC: up to 30% for solar, wind, storage
→ Clean Tech Manufacturing ITC: domestic production support
→ CCUS ITC: up to 60% for carbon capture projects

Aligning Canada with US and EU incentive structures.

---

**Tweet 9**
Major Projects Office aims to cut permitting timelines 30-50% for:
→ Large-scale nuclear (SMRs)
→ Interprovincial transmission
→ Clean hydrogen infrastructure
→ CCS facilities

But here's the issue: most won't deliver reductions until AFTER 2030.

---

**Tweet 10**

Creates a near-term gap in the critical window before 2030.
