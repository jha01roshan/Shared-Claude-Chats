I've recreated the Anthropic Office Simulator - Comedy Edition for you! This is an interactive 3D office environment where you can:

**Controls:**
- **WASD** - Move around
- **Arrow keys or mouse** - Look around  
- **E** - Interact with characters
- **SPACE** - Dance
- **ESC** - Close dialogs/release mouse

**Features:**
- Walk around a detailed office environment with desks, plants, and a coffee station
- Meet and talk to 10 different Anthropic team members, each with unique personalities and quirks
- Ask preset questions or type your own custom questions
- A chaos meter that affects the environment (things get wild at high chaos!)
- A Roomba that might start following you
- Dancing animations and random events

The characters will try to use Claude's API to generate responses to your questions, but they also have fallback responses if needed. Each character has their own personality traits - from Dario who accidentally speaks in haikus to Nova who debugs code through interpretive dance!

Have fun exploring the office and chatting with the team! The chaos level increases as you interact, leading to some amusing effects like disco mode at maximum chaos.
