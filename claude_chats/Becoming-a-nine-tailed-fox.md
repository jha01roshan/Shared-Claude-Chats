## In Mythology & Folklore

**Korean (구미호, Gumiho)**
- A fox must live for **1,000 years** to gain nine tails and transform into human form.
- They are often portrayed as shapeshifters who can appear as beautiful women.

**Japanese (狐, Kitsune)**
- Foxes gain one tail for every **100 years** of wisdom and power, up to nine.
- Nine-tailed kitsune are associated with the deity **Inari** and are considered divine messengers.

**Chinese (九尾狐, Huli Jing)**
- Ancient texts like the *Shan Hai Jing* describe them as powerful, auspicious beings.
- They could transform and were associated with good omens in early legends.
