**What the thermodynamic application proves**

1. Temperature is intensive and defined only within LTE patches (Callen, Ch. 1-3).
2. Classical thermodynamics supplies no conservation law, extremum principle, or selection rule that uniquely picks an aggregation operator for combining local intensive values over disjoint LTE patches forming a macroscopic non-equilibrium system.

Both are direct from Callen. No new physics postulated. The union of two non-overlapping equilibrium subsystems is not itself a Callen system. No single fundamental relation holds uniformly across the disjoint union. Therefore no law-selected aggregator exists.

Combined with Definition 3 (admissible mapping requires law-selected aggregation), this gives MA = ∅ for any global intensive construct on a non-equilibrium macroscopic system. By PTT, such a construct is untethered.

These are non-equivalent numbers. Thermodynamics does not privilege one. The choice is human convention.
