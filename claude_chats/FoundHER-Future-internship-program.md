Why FoundHER Future Exists
FoundHER Future was created with Black women in mind – women whose brilliance, labor, and leadership have too often been undervalued or unsupported. The program centers the upliftment, sustainability, and development of Black women, with the understanding that when Black women are resourced, held with integrity, and positioned to lead, the impact ripples outward for everyone.
Women of other racial and cultural backgrounds are welcome to apply, with the expectation that participants:

* Have awareness of social context
* Are willing to work within a Black woman–centered space
* Are aligned with the mission of contributing to the betterment and liberation of Black women as a collective good
What This Program Offers
FoundHER Future operates as an internship, combining learning with hands-on contribution. Participants will support real projects across a broader creative and community ecosystem, gaining experience that translates into future leadership, entrepreneurship, or aligned employment.
Participants can expect:

* Hands-On Experience: Real responsibilities across digital media, creative projects, administration, and systems – not busy work.
* Mentorship & Modeling: Direct guidance from experienced visionaries who teach through practice, not performance.
* Skill & Capacity Building: Communication, follow-through, collaboration, emotional integrity, and ownership.
* Personal & Professional Clarity: Growth that comes from doing meaningful work in a healthy, accountable environment.
As part of the experience, participants will also engage in the __[Discover Your Destiny™](https://dyd25.com/discover-your-destiny/)__ curriculum, a structured program designed to support purpose alignment, spiritual awareness, and intentional next steps.
Who This Is For
FoundHER Future is for women 18+ who:

* Are navigating a transition season
* Desire meaningful work with structure and accountability
* Want experience without exploitation
* Are ready to learn, contribute, and be mentored
* Are open to feedback, growth, and responsibility
This program is supportive, but not coddling. Rest and humanity are honored – but so are commitment and follow-through.

## Assistant

Here's what I'd offer:

---

**Ownership Mindset** *(consolidated)*

1. **Initiative & Intentionality** — You move without being told. You act with purpose, not just busyness.
2. **Self-Accountability** — You own your results, your mistakes, and your growth — without being managed into it.
3. **Investment & Attachment** — You genuinely care about the work, the process, *and* the outcome. It matters to you personally.
4. **Efficiency & Prioritization** — You manage your time and energy like they're resources, because they are.
5. **Global Thinking** — You see the big picture *and* the details. You understand how your piece connects to the whole.
6. **Intentional Communication** — You communicate clearly, proactively, and with the other person's understanding in mind.
7. **Reflective Growth** — You pause, assess, receive feedback, and actually apply it.
8. **Succession & Sustainability** — You build things that last beyond you. You think about what you're leaving, not just what you're doing.

---

**Employee Mindset** *(non-examples)*

1. **Waiting for Direction** — You do what you're told, when you're told, and nothing more.
2. **External Accountability Only** — You perform when someone is watching. Without follow-up, things fall through.
3. **Detachment from the Work** — It's just a task. You complete it and move on — no real stake in whether it lands well.
4. **Clock-Watching** — You work to the hour, not to the result. "That's not my job" lives here.
5. **Tunnel Vision** — You focus only on your lane, unaware of or uninterested in how it connects to the larger mission.
6. **Reactive Communication** — You respond when asked. You don't flag problems, gaps, or updates proactively.
7. **Defensive to Feedback** — Feedback feels like criticism. It's deflected, minimized, or forgotten.
8. **Replacement Mentality** — You do the job for now. No thought given to what you're building, leaving, or passing on.

---

A few things to note as you build this lesson:

- You may want to invite them to honestly self-identify which column they currently live in, without judgment, as a starting point for the program.
