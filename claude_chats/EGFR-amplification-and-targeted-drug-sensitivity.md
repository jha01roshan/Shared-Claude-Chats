## Clinical Evidence for EGFR Amplification Sensitivity (CIViC)

### **Lung Non-Small Cell Carcinoma (NSCLC)** — Strong Evidence

| Therapy | Evidence | Rating | Source |
|---------|----------|--------|--------|
| **Erlotinib + Gefitinib** | EGFR amplification associated with improved OS and PFS with TKI treatment | B/4 | [EID:5924](https://identifiers.org/civic.eid:5924), [PMID:20826716](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/20826716) |
| **EGFR TKIs (general)** | Meta-analysis: EGFR amplification associated with increased OS (HR=0.77), PFS (HR=0.60), and TTP (HR=0.50) | B/4 | [EID:948](https://identifiers.org/civic.eid:948), [PMID:20826716](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/20826716) |
| **Erlotinib** | Phase 3 trial: Significantly increased survival (HR 0.44, p=0.008) and response rate (20% vs 2%) in patients with EGFR amplification/polysomy | B/4 | [EID:7802](https://identifiers.org/civic.eid:7802), [PMID:16014883](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/16014883) |

**Note:** One study ([EID:977](https://identifiers.org/civic.eid:977)) found EGFR amplification was not independently predictive when EGFR mutations were accounted for, suggesting amplification may correlate with activating mutations.

## Summary

For tumors with **EGFR amplification**, the strongest clinical evidence supports sensitivity to:

1. **EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors (Erlotinib, Gefitinib)** in **NSCLC** — multiple meta-analyses and Phase 3 trial data support improved survival outcomes

2. **Anti-EGFR antibodies (Cetuximab, Panitumumab)** in **colorectal cancer** — high gene copy number predicts better response independent of KRAS status

3. **Cetuximab** in **gastric adenocarcinoma** — emerging evidence suggests benefit

However, EGFR amplification does **not** predict benefit from cetuximab-based therapy in **head and neck cancer** based on the EXTREME study data.
