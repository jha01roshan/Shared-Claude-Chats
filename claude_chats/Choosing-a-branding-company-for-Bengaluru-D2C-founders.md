This guide helps Bengaluru D2C founders choose a branding company, arguing that category expertise (FMCG/wellness/beauty packaging, retail-channel knowledge) matters more than physical location.

**Key needs for Bengaluru D2C brands**: speed matching startup timelines, FMCG/wellness/beauty category depth, retail channel intelligence (BigBasket, Amazon, Nykaa, Blinkit), and scalable identity systems for growing SKU portfolios.

**Market segments**: large agencies (₹15L+, corporate-focused), mid-size studios (₹5L-15L, 3-6 months), D2C specialist studios (₹2L-10L, 6-12 weeks — best value for most founders), and freelancers (single-task only).

**Five companies profiled**:
1. **Jellypop Design** – remote-first D2C specialist covering identity, packaging, and Webflow sites for FMCG/wellness/beauty (₹2L-15L)
2. **Design Stack** – mid-size studio strong in digital/UX, weaker on physical packaging
3. **Lokus Design** – mid-size, strong industrial/packaging structure, lacks full identity systems
4. **Landor** – global enterprise consultancy, not suited for D2C startups
5. **Thought Over Design** – boutique, strong visual craft, weaker on commercial retail deliverables

**Core arguments**: Location doesn't matter — category expertise and remote async processes do. Bengaluru's market has a sophisticated investor audience, Blinkit-dominant quick commerce, and crowded wellness/beauty categories, causing visual convergence (everyone looks similar). The key differentiator should be "recognizable," not just "professional."

**Complete deliverables checklist**: full identity system, print-ready/FSSAI-compliant packaging, digital/e-commerce assets, and website (if in scope).
