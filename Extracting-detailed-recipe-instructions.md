# Recipe 1: Blackened Cauliflower with Couscous and Apricot-Glazed Green Beans

## Step 1: Cook Couscous
- Preheat oven to 425 degrees
- Bring 1¾ cups water (2½ cups for 4 servings) and ½ teaspoon salt (1 teaspoon for 4) to a boil in a small pot. Once boiling, stir in **toasted pearl couscous**. Reduce heat to medium-low. Simmer 11-14 minutes, or until couscous is al dente and water has absorbed.
- Remove from heat. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon olive oil (2 tablespoons for 4). Stir to coat. (You'll finish the couscous in Step 6.)

> *If you modified your meal, refer to the cooking instructions on the CUSTOMIZE card and use them as a guide to modify your recipe as needed.*

## Step 2: Prep
- Cut **cauliflower** into bite-size pieces if necessary.
- Trim stem ends off **green beans**. Slice into pieces, about 1½ inch long each.

## Step 3: Season & Roast Cauliflower
- Place **cauliflower** in a medium bowl. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon cooking oil (2 tablespoons for 4 servings). Season with half the **blackening spice** (all for 4), ½ teaspoon salt (¾ teaspoon for 4), and a pinch of pepper. Stir to coat.
- Spread **cauliflower** out in a single layer on a lightly oiled, foil-lined baking sheet. Roast 18-20 minutes, or until fork-tender, stirring halfway through.

## Step 4: Cook Green Beans
- Heat 1½ tablespoons cooking oil in a medium sauté pan over medium heat. Add **green beans** and **sliced almonds** to hot pan. Season with ½ teaspoon salt (¾ teaspoon for 4 servings) and a pinch of pepper. Cook 4-5 minutes, or until green beans are tender, stirring occasionally.
- Remove from heat. Add **apricot jam**. Stir to coat.

## Step 5: Cook Veggies
- Heat 1½ tablespoons cooking oil in a large sauté pan over medium heat. Add **corn** and **roasted red peppers** to hot pan. Lightly season with salt and pepper. Cook 2-3 minutes, or until veggies are warmed through, stirring frequently.

## Step 6: Finish Couscous
- Transfer **couscous** to pan with **corn and roasted red peppers**. Stir to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste.*

> *Taste the couscous and veggies after seasoning and adjust as needed. Adding salt and pepper throughout the cooking process is key to a delicious dish.*

## Step 7: Plate Your Dish
- Divide **couscous and veggies** between plates. Top with **blackened cauliflower**. Drizzle with **Creole Dijonnaise** to taste. Serve **apricot-glazed green beans** on the side. Enjoy!

# Recipe 2: Turkish-Style Spiced Chickpea Stew with Almond Gremolata and Lemon Crema

## Step 1: Prep
- Cut ends off **yellow onion** and discard peel. Halve lengthwise; medium dice half the onion (whole onion for 4 servings) into pieces, about ½ inch each.
- Strain and rinse **garbanzo beans (chickpeas)**.
- Trim ends from **summer squash**: Halve lengthwise. Lay flat and slice across into half-moons, about ¼ inch thick.
- De-stem **cilantro**: roughly chop leaves.
- Zest **lemon** with a microplane (or on small holes of a box grater) over a small bowl. Quarter lemon. (For 4, zest one lemon and quarter both.)
- Finely chop **sliced almonds**.

> *If you modified your meal, refer to the cooking instructions on the CUSTOMIZE card and use them as a guide to modify your recipe as needed.*

## Step 2: Start Stew
- Heat 1½ tablespoons cooking oil in a medium pot over medium-high heat. Add **onion** and **chickpeas** to hot pot. Season with 1 teaspoon **Turkish spice blend** (2 teaspoons for 4 servings), ¼ teaspoon salt, and a pinch of pepper. Stir to combine. Cook 3-5 minutes, or until chickpeas are lightly browned, stirring occasionally.

## Step 3: Finish Stew
- Add **squash** to pot with **onion and chickpeas**. Stir to combine. Cook 1-2 minutes, stirring occasionally.
- Reduce heat to medium. Add **paprika-turmeric tomato sauce**, **veggie stock concentrate**, and about ¼ cup water (⅓ cup for 4 servings). Cook 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Remove from heat.
- Add about half the **feta cheese** and 1 tablespoon butter (2 tablespoons for 4). Stir to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Step 4: Make Almond Gremolata
- Combine **cilantro**, **almonds**, a squeeze of **lemon juice**, 2 tablespoons olive oil (4 tablespoons for 4 servings), half the **chili flakes*** (all for 4), and about ½ teaspoon sugar (1 teaspoon for 4) in a second small bowl.
- Season with salt and pepper. Stir to combine. Add more **lemon juice** to taste.

> *If heat sensitive, use chili flakes sparingly, or omit entirely.*

## Step 5: Make Lemon Crema
- Combine **sour cream**, **lemon zest** to taste, and a pinch of salt in a third small bowl. Add water 1 teaspoon at a time until mixture reaches a drizzling consistency.

## Step 6: Plate Your Dish
- Divide **Turkish-style spiced chickpea stew** between bowls. Top with **almond gremolata**. Drizzle with **lemon crema** to taste. Garnish with remaining **lemon zest** and **feta cheese**. Enjoy!
