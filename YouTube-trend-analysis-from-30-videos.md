### 2. Recurring title patterns & hooks

The highest-performing titles share consistent structural DNA:

- **"I'm [doing something unexpected] because of [force]"** — Mark Tilbury: *"I'm Changing How I Invest My Money Because of AI"* — 1.24M views. Personal confession + external catalyst.
- **Number + specific outcome** — *"$25/week into Fidelity Index Funds will SURPASS Your Full-Time Job ($4800/month)"* (132K), *"How to Start Investing With Just ₹100"* (251K). Concrete amounts outperform vague claims.
- **"NEVER [do X] before [doing Y]"** — *"NEVER Invest Before Checking These 6 Things"* — warning-framed list content.
- **"What Really Happens When…"** — *"What Really Happens When You Reach $1 Million Invested"* (77K). Curiosity gap with a milestone anchor.
- **Expert credential in title** — *"BANKER EXPLAINS"* prefix is used repeatedly by Jumoke Mi; strong authority signal.
- **"Worst/Best mistakes"** format — Graham Stephan's *"The WORST Mistakes In Personal Finance"* (64K). Negative framing ("worst") consistently outperforms positive framing.
- **Dave Ramsey as a hook** — Finance By JR's *"Dave Ramsey: 9 Tips To Live On An Extremely Low Income"* (25K) uses an established name as the subject rather than just a topic.

### 5. Opportunity gaps

These are topics where very few videos exist but the ones that do are getting outsized views:

**Gap 1: AI's impact on personal investing strategy** — 1 video in the dataset, 1.24M views. This is the biggest outlier in the entire dataset. Almost no one else is making this content yet. Mark Tilbury is essentially unchallenged in this lane.

**Gap 2: India-specific US stock market access** — 2 videos, combined 259K views, and both are from smaller/mid-tier channels (6.9M and 337K subs). Given India's enormous YouTube audience and growing retail investor class, this topic is dramatically underserved in English-language content.

**Gap 3: Housing affordability math** — Nick Invests' "How Much House Your Salary Actually Buys You" got 83.8K views and the highest likes-to-views ratio in the dataset (6.6%). Only 1 video covers this topic. In a high-rate environment, this question is extremely relevant.

**Gap 4: Finfluencer critique / media literacy** — Cara Nicole's video on this hit 69K views with 3,600 likes (5.2% ratio). Zero other videos in the dataset touch this angle. There's clear appetite for "is this financial content trustworthy?" content.

**Gap 5: Milestone-specific investing ("what happens at $X")** — Frankie Finance's $1M milestone video hit 77K views from a channel with only 6.29K subscribers — a view-to-subscriber ratio of ~12x. There's no "$10K," "$50K," or "$500K" equivalent in the dataset.

### 7. Video ideas to make right now

**Idea 1: "I Changed How I Invest Because of AI — Here's What You Should Do Too"**
Directly capitalizes on the #1 gap. A response/expansion to the Mark Tilbury format but from a mid-size creator's perspective — focus on what *regular* investors (not those with large portfolios) should actually change. The framing of personal confession + AI threat is proven to work at scale.

**Idea 2: "What Happens to Your Money At $10K, $50K, $100K, and $1M (The Compounding Ladder)"**
Extends the Frankie Finance breakout format into a ladder structure. Four milestone thresholds in one video gives YouTube algorithm multiple keyword targets and gives viewers a roadmap. The $1M framing alone drove 77K views on a 6.3K-sub channel.

**Idea 3: "How Much House Can You Actually Afford on a $[X] Salary Right Now?"**
Nick Invests proved this topic gets a 6.6% engagement ratio. A creator can make this hyper-local or hyper-specific (e.g. "$60K salary," "single income," "in 2026 with 7% rates") to own a niche the original video left open.

**Idea 4: "I Tested 5 Financial Influencers' Advice — Here's What Actually Happened"**
Cara Nicole's finfluencer critique earned 69K views and a 5.2% like ratio. An evidence-based follow-up where a creator actually tests the advice (e.g., follows Dave Ramsey's method for 30 days, tracks results) adds a layer no one in the dataset has executed yet.

**Idea 5: "How Indians Can Invest in US Stocks for Less Than ₹500 — Full 2026 Guide"**
Vaibhav Kadnar proved the India-US investing topic drives 251K views. But his video is broad. A narrowly focused, step-by-step tutorial targeting the ₹500–₹5,000 starter bracket with specific app walkthroughs (INDMoney, Groww, etc.) would capture search traffic he left on the table. Indian retail investing is one of the fastest-growing YouTube niches globally right now.
