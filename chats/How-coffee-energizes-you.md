The short version: caffeine is basically a con artist. It's shaped almost identically to adenosine — your brain's "you're tired now" molecule — so it sneaks into the same receptor slots and just... occupies them. Adenosine can't land. Your brain genuinely doesn't know it's tired. Dopamine keeps flowing. You feel sharp.

The catch: the adenosine is still building up the whole time. When caffeine wears off, all that backlogged sleepiness crashes in at once. That's the 2pm slump. That's the "why do I feel worse than before I drank it" feeling.

Your neurons aren't being energized. They're being lied to. Consistently. Every morning. And it works.
