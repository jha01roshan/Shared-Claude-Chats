Here are the two ways to hard reset the Nighthawk X8 (R8500):

---

## Method 1: Physical Reset Button (Recommended for lockouts)

1. Make sure the router is **powered on** and has fully booted (wait ~1 minute after powering on).
2. Locate the **Reset button** on the **rear panel** of the router — it's a small pinhole.
3. Use a paper clip or similar object to press and hold the Reset button for **7 seconds**, then release. The router will reset automatically.
4. Wait for the router to fully reboot (the power LED will go through its cycle).

> Some sources suggest holding up to **15 seconds until all lights flash** if 7 seconds doesn't work.

---

## Method 2: Via the Web Interface (if you can still log in)

1. Connect to the router and go to **192.168.1.1** in your browser.
2. Click **Advanced** at the top, then go to **Administration → Backup Settings**, and select **Erase**.
3. The router will revert to factory defaults and reboot.

---

## After the Reset — Default Credentials

- **Username:** `admin`
- **Password:** `password`
- **WiFi name:** `NETGEARXX` (printed on the router label)

> ⚠️ **Heads up:** A factory reset wipes all your custom settings — WiFi name, password, port forwarding rules, etc. You'll need to reconfigure everything from scratch.
