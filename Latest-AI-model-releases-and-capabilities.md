## **Gemini 3 Flash** (December 17, 2025)
Google released Gemini 3 Flash, offering frontier intelligence built for speed at a fraction of the cost. It's now the default model in the Gemini app globally, replacing Gemini 2.5 Flash. Key highlights:
- Outperforms Gemini 2.5 Pro while being 3x faster at a fraction of the cost
- Scores 90.4% on GPQA Diamond (PhD-level reasoning) and 33.7% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools
- Pricing: $0.50 per 1M input tokens, $3.00 per 1M output tokens
- SWE-bench Verified score of 78% for agentic coding, outperforming Gemini 3 Pro

---

## **GPT-5.2 & GPT-5.2-Codex** (December 11-18, 2025)
OpenAI introduced GPT-5.2, calling it the most capable model series yet for professional knowledge work. Notable features:
- Better at creating spreadsheets, presentations, writing code, perceiving images, and handling complex multi-step projects
- Knowledge cutoff moved to August 31, 2025 (significant jump from prior models)
- GPT-5.2 Thinking responses contain 38% fewer errors than predecessors
- GPT-5.2-Codex was released December 18 as the most advanced agentic coding model, with significantly stronger cybersecurity capabilities

---

## **GPT Image 1.5** (December 16, 2025)
OpenAI released a new version of ChatGPT Images promising better instruction-following, more precise editing, and up to 4x faster image generation speeds.
- Makes precise edits while keeping details like facial likeness, lighting, composition, and color tone consistent across edits
- Originally slated for January 2026 but was rushed to compete with Google's Nano Banana Pro

---

## **Mistral OCR 3** (December 17, 2025)
Mistral AI released OCR 3 with breakthrough performance: 74% overall win rate over Mistral OCR 2 on forms, scanned documents, complex tables, and handwriting.
- Available at $2 per 1,000 pages (50% discount with Batch API makes it $1)
- Supports markdown output with HTML-based table reconstruction
- Dramatically undercuts AWS Textract and Google Document AI pricing

---

## **NVIDIA Nemotron 3** (December 15, 2025)
NVIDIA announced the Nemotron 3 family of open models designed for transparent, efficient, and specialized agentic AI development.
- Three sizes: Nano (30B params/3B active), Super (~100B/10B active), Ultra (~500B/50B active)
- Uses hybrid Mamba-Transformer MoE architecture with 1M token context length
- Nemotron 3 Nano delivers 4x higher throughput than Nemotron 2 Nano
- Super and Ultra arriving in first half of 2026

---

## **Grok Voice Agent API** (December 17, 2025)
xAI launched the Grok Voice Agent API, enabling developers to build voice agents that speak dozens of languages, call tools, and search realtime data.
- Ranks #1 on Big Bench Audio with average time-to-first-audio of less than 1 second—nearly 5x faster than closest competitors
- Simple flat rate pricing of $0.05 per minute
- Compatible with OpenAI Realtime API specification and available via xAI LiveKit Plugin

---

## **Claude Organization Skills** (December 18, 2025)
Anthropic made Skills easier to deploy, discover, and build with organization-wide management for admins, a directory of partner-built skills from Notion, Canva, Figma, Atlassian, and others, and published Agent Skills as an open standard.
- Team and Enterprise admins can now manage Skills centrally
- Agent Skills is now an open standard, making skills portable across tools like ChatGPT or Cursor that adopt it

---

## **Meta SAM Audio** (December 16, 2025)
Meta introduced SAM Audio, the first unified AI model that can segment sound from complex audio mixtures using text, visual, and time span prompts.
- Three prompt types: text ("dog barking"), visual (click on person/object in video), and span (mark time segments)
- Available in three sizes: sam-audio-small, sam-audio-base, and sam-audio-large
- Open source and available in the Segment Anything Playground

---

## **Budget-Aware Agent Scaling Study** (November 2025)
This research paper addresses how to scale tool-augmented agents effectively under explicit tool-call budgets. The key finding: simply granting agents a larger tool-call budget fails to improve performance because they lack "budget awareness."
- Introduces BATS (Budget Aware Test-time Scaling), a framework that dynamically adapts planning and verification strategy based on remaining budget
- The "physics law" reference in the newsletter likely refers to the discovery of log-linear scaling relationships between performance and compute budget
