OpenAI is a private company.

#### Founding Year and Headquarters  
Founded in 2015, OpenAI is headquartered in San Francisco, California.

#### Founders  
OpenAI was co-founded by a group of notable individuals including Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, Wojciech Zaremba, and Elon Musk.  
- **Sam Altman**: From St. Louis, Missouri, Altman studied computer science at Stanford University (though he dropped out to pursue entrepreneurship). He is best known for being president of Y Combinator, where he mentored and funded numerous successful startups, and for his early success with Loopt, a location-based social networking app sold for $43 million in 2012. His vision for scalable AI innovation drives OpenAI’s commercial success.  
- **Greg Brockman**: Originally from North Dakota, Brockman attended Harvard University and later MIT, though he left to dive into tech. He was CTO of Stripe, where he helped build the payment platform into a fintech giant, bringing technical leadership to OpenAI’s infrastructure scaling.  
- **Ilya Sutskever**: Born in Russia and raised in Israel and Canada, Sutskever earned a Ph.D. from the University of Toronto under AI pioneer Geoffrey Hinton. A leading researcher in deep learning, he co-invented AlexNet, a breakthrough neural network, and his work shapes OpenAI’s cutting-edge AI models.  
- **Wojciech Zaremba**: From Poland, Zaremba holds a Ph.D. from New York University and worked as a researcher at Google Brain. His expertise in reinforcement learning bolsters OpenAI’s algorithmic advancements.  
- **Elon Musk**: Born in South Africa, Musk studied physics and economics at the University of Pennsylvania. A serial entrepreneur, he founded Tesla, SpaceX, and co-founded PayPal, driving OpenAI’s early mission to advance AGI for humanity.  
Their collective achievements in AI research, tech entrepreneurship, and scaling businesses underpin OpenAI’s rapid rise.
