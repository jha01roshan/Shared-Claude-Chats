### Quick Grammar 101 (Why the comma *can* appear — but you don’t need it)

> **With comma (formal, appositive):**  
> *Florida Surgeon General, Dr. Joseph Ladapo, champions...*  
> → Treats "Dr. Joseph Ladapo" as a **non-restrictive appositive** (extra info about the title).

> **Without comma (modern, clean, meme-friendly):**  
> **Florida Surgeon General Dr. Joseph Ladapo champions...**  
> → This is **AP Style**, **BBC**, **X/Twitter**, and **most headlines**. No comma. Cleaner. Faster to read.
