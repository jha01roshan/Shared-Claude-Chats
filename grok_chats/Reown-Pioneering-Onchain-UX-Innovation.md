## Product
Reown offers an onchain UX platform with toolkits like AppKit (for dApps) and WalletKit (for wallets), built on the WalletConnect Network. The unique innovation is an open-source, secure protocol for effortless connections between dApps and wallets across chains (EVM, Solana, Bitcoin), enabling intuitive digital ownership without exposing private keys.

This helps customers by simplifying integrations for developers (reducing overhead by 99%), allowing faster launches and scaling. Users benefit from frictionless experiences like one-click auth, multi-wallet support, and embedded payments, making onchain operations more efficient, cost-effective (low/no fees for core use), and painless compared to fragmented alternatives.
