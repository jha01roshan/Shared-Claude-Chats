**Yes, FTX customers (and most non-governmental creditors) are getting paid back in full—plus interest—through the Chapter 11 bankruptcy process.** This is one of the stronger recoveries in a major bankruptcy, though not everyone is fully satisfied due to how claims were valued.<grok:render card_id="7b89a6" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">6</argument></grok:render>

### Key Details on Repayments
- **Recovery Rate**: The FTX estate (under CEO John Ray III after the 2022 collapse) recovered enough assets (~$14.7–$16.5 billion) to repay customers **~118–119%** of their allowed claims (valued in USD as of the November 2022 petition date). This covers 100% principal plus interest for non-governmental creditors.<grok:render card_id="7d58fb" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">38</argument></grok:render>
- **Who Gets Paid**: About **98% of customers** (those with claims ≤ $50,000, the "Convenience Class") were prioritized for early payouts. Larger claims and other creditors follow in phased distributions.<grok:render card_id="38feb3" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">1</argument></grok:render>
- **Timeline and Distributions** (as of mid-2026):
  - Plan confirmed in October 2024; effective January 3, 2025.
  - Multiple rounds have occurred: Initial payouts in early 2025 (e.g., ~$1.2B+), followed by billions more in May, September 2025, and March 2026 (~$2.2B in the fourth round alone).
  - Cumulative distributions have reached ~$10 billion by early 2026, with more planned.<grok:render card_id="888c84" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">2</argument></grok:render>
  - Funds distributed via partners like BitGo, Kraken, or Payoneer after KYC/verification.

This is handled by the **FTX Recovery Trust** under court oversight—not direct "government takeover," though the U.S. Bankruptcy Court approved the plan and the DOJ/others were involved in asset recoveries.<grok:render card_id="958e27" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">35</argument></grok:render>

### Important Caveats
- **Dollar Value vs. Crypto Value**: Claims were "dollarized" at November 2022 prices (e.g., Bitcoin ~$16k–$17k). Many customers missed out on later crypto gains (e.g., BTC now much higher), leading to complaints that it's not a "full" recovery in asset terms. Legally and in fiat, it's more than whole.<grok:render card_id="53a9a8" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">15</argument></grok:render>
- **Not Everyone**: Governmental claims (e.g., IRS, SEC fines) and certain insiders/executives may differ. SBF and key associates faced criminal consequences separately; some assets were clawed back. Preferred equity holders (like shareholders) get later/lower priority payments.<grok:render card_id="ffaf66" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">2</argument></grok:render>
- **Process**: Eligible claimants must verify via the FTX claims portal and meet requirements. Scams are common—only use official channels.

In short, **yes for the vast majority of customers** ("SBF's people" likely meaning FTX users), this is a rare near-full recovery in bankruptcy. Check the official Kroll restructuring site or claims.ftx.com for personal status.
