**Ornn Compute Price Index (OCPI)**: This is Ornn’s flagship benchmark family for the input side of the AI economy, tracking live **spot prices** for GPU compute hours across major hardware types (e.g., OCPI-H100 SXM, H200, B200, RTX 5090, and others). Built exclusively from executed, printed transactions (not scraped offers, rate cards, surveys, or estimates), it normalizes pricing across providers, configurations, regions (with geographic weighting, e.g., US-East vs. EU-West), and deployment contexts to create standardized, comparable reference rates typically expressed in **USD per GPU-hour**. Updated frequently (e.g., intraday or daily), it serves as the foundational settlement index for derivatives, swaps, and futures (including planned ICE GPU compute futures), enabling price discovery, hedging, volatility tracking, and financing in an otherwise opaque, non-fungible market. **Ornn Token Price Indices (OTPI)**: Launched June 16, 2026, this new family benchmarks the **output side**—the realized cost of AI inference tokens from frontier model providers.
