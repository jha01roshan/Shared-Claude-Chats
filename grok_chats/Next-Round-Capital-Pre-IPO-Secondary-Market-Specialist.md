### Role in Secondary Market Sales (from a VC Perspective)
In the VC-backed private company ecosystem, Next Round Capital serves as a critical **specialized intermediary and liquidity provider** in the growing secondary market. Companies are staying private longer (often as unicorns or “decacorns”), which traditionally locks up capital for VCs, employees, and early shareholders. Secondaries address this by allowing share sales *without* an IPO, acquisition, or tender offer—providing earlier liquidity, price discovery, and capital recycling.<grok:render card_id="a237f0" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">12</argument></grok:render>

**From a pure VC lens**, their role is highly valuable for several reasons:
- **Portfolio Management & Returns Realization**: VCs can sell portions of holdings in high-performing portfolio companies (e.g., via direct cap-table transactions) to lock in gains, return capital to LPs sooner, or recycle into new investments—without forcing a full exit or waiting years for an IPO. This is especially useful in volatile or extended private-market cycles.
- **LP and Employee Liquidity**: Helps VCs support their portfolio companies by facilitating employee/option-holder liquidity (improving retention and morale) or partial LP secondaries. It also aids in fund-level liquidity management.
- **Market Intelligence & Execution Edge**: With real-time pricing, a broad buyer network (hundreds of institutions), and expertise in navigating complexities (e.g., ROFRs, lock-ups, securities laws), they enable efficient, discreet deals that traditional brokers or platforms might not access at scale.
