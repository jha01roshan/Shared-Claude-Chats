## Assistant

That quote captures Elon Musk's audacious mindset perfectly. Instead of chasing luxury, he’s betting big on humanity’s survival and expansion into space. The idea stems from his belief that becoming a multi-planetary species is critical to avoiding extinction—whether from asteroid impacts, climate collapse, or other existential risks. His friends’ skepticism reflects how wild the idea sounded back then, but SpaceX’s progress, like landing Falcon 9 boosters or planning Starship missions, shows he’s not just dreaming. Mars is his hedge against humanity’s fragility, a way to ensure consciousness endures. It’s classic Musk: high stakes, higher purpose.
