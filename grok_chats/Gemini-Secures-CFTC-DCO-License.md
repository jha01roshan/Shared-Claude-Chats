### What a DCO License Means
A DCO acts as a central counterparty, substituting its credit for the parties involved and mutualizing/transferring risk. This is a major regulatory milestone for crypto firms, as only about 22 entities in the U.S. hold DCO status. It reduces reliance on third-party clearers (e.g., QC Clearing LLC), lowers costs, speeds up processes, and supports expansion into prediction markets and potentially broader crypto derivatives like perps or futures.<grok:render card_id="8744a7" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">40</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="f8ea1a" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">18</argument></grok:render>

Gemini has been building a full CFTC "stack" (DCM for trading + DCO for clearing; FCM registration likely next). This positions it alongside a small group of crypto-native players like **Bitnomial** (full stack) and **Crypto.com** (which achieved a similar full stack earlier). Traditional players like Coinbase and Kraken are pursuing acquisitions or partnerships for derivatives capabilities.<grok:render card_id="6a973d" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">60</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="abd77f" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">41</argument></grok:render>
