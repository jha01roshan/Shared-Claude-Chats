## Assistant

Yes, the Exothermic Core-Mantle Decoupling – Dzhanibekov Oscillation (ECDO) Hypothesis suggests sudden changes in Earth's rotation. It proposes a periodic, violent process where the Earth's core decouples from the mantle and crust, leading to a rapid 104-degree shift in the planet's rotational axis along the 31st East meridian toward South Africa.
