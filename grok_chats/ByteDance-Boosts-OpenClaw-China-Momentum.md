### What ClawHub and OpenClaw Actually Are

It runs locally or in the cloud, executes real-world tasks (file ops, software control, workflows), integrates with chat apps, and uses any LLM backend. OpenClaw is the de facto open standard right now — not a VC-backed startup per se, but the platform around which dozens of emerging private companies (hosted agents, enterprise management layers, security/compliance tools, vertical applications, skill creators, managed infra) are building.

**Positive momentum drivers for OpenClaw/ClawHub ecosystem:**
- **China market flywheel supercharged**: China is currently ground zero for agent adoption (viral consumer craze + gov subsidies + corporate experiments). The official mirror removes friction for Chinese devs/users, driving more skill publication, more agent installs, and stronger network effects. ByteDance backing adds credibility and performance that an unofficial mirror couldn’t match.
