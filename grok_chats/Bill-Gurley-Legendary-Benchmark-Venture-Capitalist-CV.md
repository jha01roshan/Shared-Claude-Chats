**John William “Bill” Gurley**  
**Curriculum Vitae** (as of March 2026)  

**Professional Summary**  
Legendary venture capitalist and longest-serving General Partner at Benchmark Capital (1999–2020). Built an extraordinary track record backing category-defining technology companies that delivered multiple IPOs, blockbuster acquisitions, and outsized returns for investors. Early lead investor and board member of Uber (one of the highest-returning venture investments in history). Additional landmark bets include Zillow, Grubhub, OpenTable, Stitch Fix, and Nextdoor.  

Transitioned from top-ranked Wall Street technology research analyst (Institutional Investor All-American Research Team, 1995–1996) and practicing computer engineer. Recognized thought leader on technology business models, capital markets, and startup economics via the long-running blog *Above the Crowd*. Frequent speaker, Forbes Midas List honoree, and TechCrunch Crunchies “VC of the Year” (2016). Currently author and founder of philanthropic and policy initiatives focused on career development and opportunity expansion.
