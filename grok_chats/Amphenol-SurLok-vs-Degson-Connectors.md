### Amphenol SurLok Overview
Amphenol SurLok, particularly the SurLok Plus™ series, is a line of high-performance, field-installable compression lugs and connectors designed by Amphenol Industrial Operations, a division of Amphenol Corporation. These connectors are renowned for their reliability, ease of use, and advanced features like the R4 RADSOK® technology, which enhances conductivity and reduces insertion force. They are IP67-rated, support high voltage (up to 1500VDC) and current (50A to 500A), and are widely used in applications such as electric vehicles (EVs), energy storage systems, and heavy industrial equipment. The SurLok Plus series emphasizes quick-lock mechanisms, touch-safe designs, and compatibility with standard crimp, screw, and busbar terminations, eliminating the need for specialized tools.

### Degson Overview
Degson Electronics, a Chinese manufacturer, specializes in a broad range of connectors, including terminal blocks, circular connectors, heavy-duty connectors (HDC), and energy storage connectors. Degson has recently gained attention for its 50A–350A high-protection energy storage connectors, designed to meet the UL4128 standard, targeting modular battery systems for EVs and industrial energy storage. Degson’s products are known for their cost-effectiveness, wide availability, and certifications like ISO 9001 and UL, appealing to both industrial and DIY markets.
