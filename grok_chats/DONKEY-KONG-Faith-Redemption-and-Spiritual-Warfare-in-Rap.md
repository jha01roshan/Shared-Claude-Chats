The artists use vivid imagery, cultural references, and metaphors to convey their commitment to living righteously, finding peace in faith, and overcoming spiritual battles with divine support. 3. **Strength Through Faith**: The song portrays God and the Holy Spirit as sources of strength, protection, and guidance, replacing the need for worldly defenses like guns or bravado.
4. **Redemption and Forgiveness**: The artists highlight God's forgiveness and their ability to forgive others, reflecting a Christ-like approach to relationships and personal growth.
5. **Spiritual Warfare**: References to battling Satan and overcoming temptation underscore the idea of faith as a weapon against evil influences.

The song uses contemporary rap vernacular and imagery (e.g., "Donkey Kong," "Glock," "NFL") to connect with a modern audience while delivering a Christian message. - **Analysis**: The "Wizard of Oz" alludes to deceptive or illusory forces (possibly Satan or worldly temptations) trying to steal his "Rock," a metaphor for Jesus Christ, who is often described as a foundation or cornerstone in Christian theology. "Nah" asserts his refusal to let this happen.
