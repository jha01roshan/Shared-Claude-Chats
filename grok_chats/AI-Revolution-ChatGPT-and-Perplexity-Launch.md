In 2022, the artificial intelligence landscape underwent a seismic shift with the rapid emergence of generative AI tools that brought advanced capabilities directly to the public. Prior to this, AI was largely confined to research labs, enterprise applications, and niche tech communities. However, the back-to-back launches of OpenAI's ChatGPT and Perplexity AI's search engine in late 2022 marked a turning point, democratizing AI and igniting global conversations about its transformative potential—and its risks. These events unfolded against a backdrop of accelerating AI development, fueled by advancements in large language models (LLMs) like GPT-3, but it was the user-friendly interfaces of these products that captured widespread attention.
