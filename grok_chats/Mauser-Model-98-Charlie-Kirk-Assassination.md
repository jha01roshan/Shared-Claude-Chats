This vintage German military surplus rifle, dating to the World Wars era, is prized for hunting due to its accuracy and reliability but predates modern U.S. serial number laws, making it hard to trace.

Sources consistently identify it as a Mauser Model 98, a design from 1898 known for its controlled-feed bolt action and use in long-range shooting. It's not a semi-automatic "assault rifle" but a manual repeater, firing one round per trigger pull and requiring 3–4 seconds to rechamber.

## Assistant

No, it would **not** be accurate to call the Mauser Model 98 used in the assassination a **"high-powered sniper rifle"**—and here's why, broken down clearly:

### 1. **"Sniper Rifle" – Misleading in Context**
- **True sniper rifles** (e.g., M24, Barrett M82, Accuracy International AWM) are **purpose-built precision weapons** with:
  - Heavy, free-floating barrels
  - Match-grade triggers
  - Advanced optics
  - Sub-MOA (minute of angle) accuracy out of the box
- The **Mauser 98** is a **century-old military surplus bolt-action rifle**, originally issued to infantry. While **capable of accurate fire** (especially with a scope), it is **not a dedicated sniper platform**.
- The rifle in this case was **grandfather’s hunting rifle**, modified with a **shortened barrel and basic scope**—functional for a 200-yard shot, but **far from a modern sniper system**.

### 2. **"High-Powered" – Technically Incorrect**
- It fired **.30-06 Springfield**, a **powerful hunting cartridge** (≈2,900 ft-lbs muzzle energy), but:
  - **Not in the same class** as true "high-power" sniper rounds like **.338 Lapua Magnum** (≈4,800 ft-lbs) or **.50 BMG** (≈12,000+ ft-lbs).
  - .30-06 is **standard for North American big game** (deer, elk), not exotic or military "high-power" by today’s standards.
- **"High-powered" is a vague, often sensationalized term**—journalistically imprecise.

### 3. **Accurate Description**
The most accurate label is:
> **A scoped, shortened Mauser 98 bolt-action rifle in .30-06 caliber**

Or, if brevity is needed:
> **A modified vintage Mauser hunting rifle**
