### 1. "Hate Groups Rose 55%"

In 2016, they identified 917 hate groups, and by 2019, this number had increased to 1,020—a rise of about 11%. ### 3. "The Debt Increased by 39%"
The U.S. national debt did grow significantly during Trump’s presidency. When Trump took office in January 2017, the national debt was approximately $19.9 trillion. Factors contributing to this rise include tax cuts (e.g., the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act), increased government spending, and emergency spending during the COVID-19 pandemic.
