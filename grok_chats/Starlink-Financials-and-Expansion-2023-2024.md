# Summary of Starlink Satellite Services Corp. Consolidated Financial Statements (2024 and 2023)

## Overview
Starlink Satellite Services Corp. (SSSC), a wholly owned subsidiary of Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX), operates as an intermediate holding company providing high-speed, low-latency broadband internet through the Starlink Network. Incorporated in Delaware in 2019 and reorganized in Texas in 2024, SSSC expanded its operations from 91 to 118 countries between 2023 and 2024.

## Financial Performance
- **Revenue**: Increased by 93% from $1.399 billion in 2023 to $2.708 billion in 2024, driven by market expansion and recurring service fees from Starlink Kits.
- **Net Income/Loss**: Achieved a net income of $72.7 million in 2024, compared to a net loss of $30.7 million in 2023, due to improved cost management and higher sales.
- **Cash and Equivalents**: Decreased by $19.3 million to $128.6 million in 2024, primarily due to $180 million used in operating activities, $75.5 million for property and equipment, and $7.2 million for related party debt repayment.
- **Accounts Receivable**: Grew by $8.1 million to $204.9 million in 2024, reflecting a 99% revenue increase and collection timing.
- **Inventory**: Rose by $87.6 million to $157.9 million in 2024 to support expansion into new markets.
- **Shareholder's Equity**: Shifted from a $8.5 million deficit in 2023 to $2 million in retained earnings in 2024.

## Operations and Risks
- **Business Model**: SSSC distributes Starlink Kits and broadband services globally, relying on SpaceX for network infrastructure and equipment. Revenue comes from hardware sales and monthly service fees.
- **Market Risks**: Faces competition from other satellite and terrestrial internet providers, foreign currency fluctuations, and regulatory challenges.
- **Credit Risk**: Manages credit exposure through customer evaluations, with no significant risk reported for cash and equivalents as of December 31, 2024.
- **Currency Risk**: Bears foreign exchange risk due to transactions in local currencies while purchasing from SpaceX in U.S. dollars, though impacts have been minimal.
- **Legal and Regulatory**: No material legal proceedings reported, but future regulatory changes could affect operations.

## Strategic Outlook
- **Expansion Plans**: Aims to enter new markets in 2025 and 2026, requiring investment in additional gateway sites and financial arrangements with SpaceX.
- **Workforce**: Employed 14 staff as of December 31, 2024, with plans to hire more globally in 2025 and 2026.
- **Sustainability**: Aligns with SpaceX’s commitment to safe, reliable satellite operations, with Starlink satellites achieving over 99% reliability.

## Audit and Compliance
- **Audit Opinion**: PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP audited the 2024 and 2023 financial statements, confirming they fairly present SSSC’s financial position under U.S. GAAP.
- **Equal Employment**: Adheres to SpaceX’s Equal Employment Opportunity Policy, with a gender-balanced board.
- **Subsequent Events**: No significant events reported from January 1, 2025, to June 27, 2025.
