### Core Technology and Value Proposition

Vana is an EVM-compatible Layer-1 blockchain purpose-built for private, user-owned data. Users keep their raw data in personal servers (or self-custodied nodes), grant permissioned access via the protocol, and pool it into **DataDAOs**—community-governed organizations that turn fragmented personal histories (Reddit posts, LinkedIn activity, credit-card spends, Netflix binges, you name it) into verifiable, high-signal datasets for AI training. Contributors earn ownership stakes in the resulting models and ongoing rewards through Data Liquidity Pools.

The industry problem it torches: AI has run out of public internet slop to train on, yet 99% of the world’s highest-quality behavioral data sits trapped in Big Tech silos. Vana’s fix is radical portability + cryptographic proof-of-contribution + onchain incentives. Target market? The exploding AI data economy—trillions in projected value as models get hungrier and regulators get stricter.
