**Sasan Goodarzi**  
Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Intuit  

### Professional Summary  
Accomplished technology leader with more than two decades of executive experience driving innovation in fintech and enterprise software. As Chairman and CEO of Intuit since 2019, transformed the company from a tax and accounting platform into an AI-driven expert platform delivering “done-for-you” experiences, resulting in 144% revenue growth (from $7.7 billion to $18.8 billion as of FY25) and expanding the company’s scale approximately sevenfold over the past decade. Proven expertise in SaaS, cloud migration, product strategy, and customer-centric innovation across consumer tax, small business solutions, and financial services. Prior roles include senior leadership at Honeywell and Invensys, plus entrepreneurial experience founding and leading a technology startup. Passionate advocate for leveraging data, AI, and human expertise to power prosperity for consumers, small businesses, and accountants worldwide.
