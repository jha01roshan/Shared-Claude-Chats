### Fact-Based Conclusions

Discrimination against Asians has been substantiated through statistical disparities and lower subjective ratings, though often framed as "negative action" rather than explicit quotas. The 2023 ruling has led to modest Asian gains at tech-focused elites like MIT, but overall trends are inconsistent, with drops in underrepresented minority enrollment. Historical arguments highlight Asians' dual role as both beneficiaries and victims of affirmative action, with no clear resolution on whether biases persist via non-racial proxies.
