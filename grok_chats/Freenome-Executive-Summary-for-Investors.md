#### Product and Services  
Freenome develops **blood-based tests for early cancer detection**, focusing initially on colorectal cancer with plans to expand to other cancer types. The unique innovation lies in its **multiomics platform**, which combines machine learning, genomics, and proteomics to detect cancer signals in the blood long before symptoms appear.  
- **Customer Benefits**:  
  - **Efficiency**: Non-invasive tests reduce the need for complex procedures like colonoscopies.  
  - **Cost**: Early detection lowers treatment costs by identifying cancer at manageable stages.  
  - **Reduced Pain**: Blood tests eliminate the discomfort and risks of traditional screening methods.
