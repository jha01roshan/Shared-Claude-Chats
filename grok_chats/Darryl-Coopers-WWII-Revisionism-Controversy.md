### Why "revisionist" (and why the backlash from historians, media, and others)?

Historical revisionism simply means re-examining established narratives with new evidence or angles. But the term is often used critically when claims appear to ignore, downplay, or selectively reinterpret overwhelming primary-source evidence in ways that serve an ideological agenda. Critics across the political spectrum (including historians like Andrew Roberts and Niall Ferguson, Bari Weiss, and Jewish organizations) argue Cooper's take fits the latter category for these reasons:

- **It flips the documented aggressor narrative**: Decades of archives (Nazi records, Hitler's *Mein Kampf* and speeches, Wehrmacht orders, Nuremberg evidence, British/French/Soviet documents) show Hitler pursued deliberate expansionism and total war. He violated the Munich Agreement, invaded Poland (with the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact enabling it), planned Operation Barbarossa for Lebensraum in the East, and had no genuine interest in lasting peace with Britain after conquering Western Europe. Churchill's "never surrender" stance and rejection of a 1940 armistice are seen as preventing Nazi domination of Europe—not starting the wider war. Cooper's timeline and causality (e.g., blaming Churchill for escalation while minimizing Hitler's pre-1939 actions) are viewed as ahistorical or conspiratorial.<grok:render card_id="ddacbf" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">20</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="543f51" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">11</argument></grok:render>

- **It minimizes Nazi crimes, especially the Holocaust**: The "logistical accident" framing of camp deaths contradicts explicit Nazi planning (Wannsee Conference, Einsatzgruppen reports, gas chamber blueprints, Himmler's orders, and survivor/ perpetrator testimonies). This echoes fringe Holocaust revisionism, which is why it drew accusations of "Nazi apologia" or "Holocaust revisionism" from outlets like *The New York Times*, *The Free Press*, and Democratic lawmakers.<grok:render card_id="de3ac8" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">35</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="58922a" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">17</argument></grok:render>

That said, the "many" who call him revisionist do so because his specific claims don't hold up against the archival record—they shift primary blame from the regime that started the war and industrialized genocide onto a British leader who rallied resistance.
