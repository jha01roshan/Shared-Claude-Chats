## Professional Summary
Marcelo Claure is a Bolivian-American entrepreneur, investor, and technology executive with over 25 years of experience in telecommunications, technology, and global investments. He founded Brightstar Corporation in 1997, growing it into the world's largest wireless distribution and services company with operations in over 50 countries and revenues exceeding $10 billion by 2013. As CEO of Sprint, he orchestrated a turnaround from annual losses to profitability, culminating in a $26 billion merger with T-Mobile in 2020, creating the world's most valuable telecommunications company. At SoftBank Group International, he managed a $450 billion portfolio and launched the $8 billion SoftBank Latin America Fund, the largest dedicated to the region. Currently, as Founder and CEO of Claure Group, he deploys multi-billion-dollar investments across AI, technology, climate transition, and entertainment. He serves as Chairman of Bicycle Capital, Vice Chairman of SHEIN, Co-Chairman of Brightstar Capital Partners, and Co-Chair of Harvard's D^3 Institute. His investments include leading AI firms like OpenAI, Anthropic, and SpaceX, with a focus on innovation, Latin American growth, and underrepresented founders.
