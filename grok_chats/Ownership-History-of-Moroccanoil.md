## Assistant

**Moroccanoil** is a privately held cosmetics company specializing in argan oil-infused hair and body care products. It was founded in 2008 by Carmen Tal and her then-husband Ofer Tal after they discovered argan oil's benefits during a trip to Israel. Originally started by Israeli brothers Mike and Erik Sabag in 2003, the Tals acquired the company in 2008 and relocated production to a facility in northern Israel.

The brand remains under the ownership of **Moroccanoil Israel, Ltd.**, an Israeli entity headquartered in New York City, with no indications of acquisition by a larger corporation as of 2025. Carmen Tal continues to be closely associated with the brand as a co-founder and influential figure, though specific current ownership shares are not publicly detailed due to its private status.

## Assistant

Yes, Moroccanoil has been sponsoring the Eurovision Song Contest as its "Presenting Partner" since 2020. The partnership, initially announced in 2019 for a multi-year deal through at least 2024, has been renewed multiple times, including for 2025 and extending into 2026. As part of this role, the company provides professional hairstylists to style performers, hosts, and participants during rehearsals and live shows, and its turquoise branding has influenced elements like the event's "turquoise carpet" (formerly the red carpet).

The sponsorship has faced controversy, particularly since the 2023 Israel-Gaza conflict, with petitions and public backlash calling for its removal due to Moroccanoil's Israeli ownership, though it has continued uninterrupted.
