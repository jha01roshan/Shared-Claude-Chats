## Assistant

Yes, Archbishop Timothy P. Broglio served in the U.S. Navy as a chaplain for over 23 years (1983–2001, with later reserve service).

He entered the U.S. Navy Chaplain Corps immediately after his priestly ordination in 1977 (initially as a chaplain candidate while studying in Rome, then full active duty from 1983). During his military career he attained the rank of **Captain** (O-6) and served in various assignments, including with the U.S. Marine Corps and at Naval Support Activity Naples, Italy. He retired from the active chaplaincy in 2001 when he was appointed Apostolic Nuncio, but remained in the Navy Reserve until at least 2013.

His military service is one of the reasons he was elected in 2022 as President of the U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops with the strong support of bishops who are also military or veteran chaplains, and he continues to serve as the Archbishop for the Military Services, USA (appointed in 2008).
