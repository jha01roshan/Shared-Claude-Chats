#### AI Techniques in Particle Physics
Particle physics, particularly at facilities like CERN's Large Hadron Collider, generates vast datasets requiring advanced AI for analysis. Key techniques include:

- **Deep Learning for Particle Identification:** Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) are pivotal for image processing and time series analysis, respectively. For instance, CNNs are used for event classification in collider data, as noted in a CERN article ([CERN](https://home.cern/news/news/physics/how-can-ai-help-physicists-search-new-particles)). RNNs handle sequential data, enhancing particle tracking accuracy.

- **Generative AI for Simulation:** Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) simulate particle collisions, aiding in data augmentation and validation. GANs, for example, generate realistic particle interaction scenarios, as discussed in a Symmetry magazine guide ([Symmetry Magazine](https://www.symmetrymagazine.org/article/symmetrys-guide-to-ai-in-particle-physics-and-astrophysics?language_content_entity=und)).

- **AI Models Respecting Symmetries:** Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) and symmetry-aware models incorporate gauge symmetries, crucial for high-energy physics. A MIT study demonstrated PINNs achieving efficiency gains in simulations, respecting these symmetries ([MIT Physics](https://physics.mit.edu/news/provably-exact-artificial-intelligence-for-nuclear-and-particle-physics/)).

- **Anomaly Detection in Particle Data:** Techniques like One-class SVMs and Autoencoders detect rare events, essential for discovering new particles. Autoencoders, for instance, compress data to identify anomalies, as highlighted in a US Particle Physics brochure ([US Particle Physics](https://www.usparticlephysics.org/brochure/particle-physicists-advance-artificial-intelligence/)).

#### AI Techniques in Cosmology
Cosmology deals with the universe's structure and evolution, leveraging AI for data-intensive tasks:

- **Machine Learning in Cosmology:** CNNs classify galaxies and analyze shapes, while Bayesian neural networks estimate cosmological parameters with high precision. A Carnegie Mellon University news article detailed AI accelerating simulations, reducing computation time significantly ([Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/news/stories/archives/2021/may/machine-learning-cosmology.html)).

- **AI for Accelerating Simulations:** AI models, such as neural networks, speed up cosmological simulations, enabling researchers to model large-scale structures efficiently. This is evident in studies using machine learning to predict universe evolution, as seen in a Space.com article ([Space](https://www.space.com/artificial-intelligence-universe-settings-cosmological-parameters)).

#### AI Techniques in Quantum Physics
Quantum physics, focusing on the smallest scales, benefits from AI in modeling and optimization:

- **AI in Quantum Physics:** Neural networks model quantum states, with machine learning aiding quantum error correction. Quantum machine learning algorithms leverage quantum computing, as discussed in a Scientific American article on AI designing quantum experiments ([Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-designs-quantum-physics-experiments-beyond-what-any-human-has-conceived/)).

- **Quantum-Inspired Techniques:** Quantum-inspired tensor networks and quantum neural networks extend classical AI, exploiting quantum properties for enhanced computation. These are detailed in an arXiv paper on AI in physics ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.01023)).

#### AI Techniques in Theoretical Physics
Theoretical physics uses AI for mathematical discovery and problem-solving:

- **Physics-Informed Machine Learning:** PINNs solve differential equations, while AI assists in symbolic computation, discovering new physical laws. A Nature Reviews Physics article highlighted PINNs integrating data and models ([Nature Reviews Physics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42254-021-00314-5)).

- **AI for Theoretical Discoveries:** Machine learning for mathematical proof searching and AI-assisted symbolic regression uncover new patterns, as noted in a Quanta Magazine piece on physics-inspired AI ([Quanta Magazine](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-physics-principle-that-inspired-modern-ai-art-20230105/)).

- **Reinforcement Learning and Specialized Algorithms:** Techniques like Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO) and Divide-and-Conquer RL optimize in physics environments, with SciNet and the AI Physicist Algorithm mimicking scientific discovery. These are detailed in the arXiv review paper ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.01023)), which includes experiments like inferring theories for 2D ball motion.
