### Background and Story

Tesla's lithium refinery, located near Corpus Christi, Texas, represents a strategic move to vertically integrate its battery supply chain and reduce reliance on foreign sources, particularly China's dominant position in global lithium processing. The project was first announced in 2022 as part of Tesla's broader efforts to secure critical minerals for electric vehicles (EVs) and energy storage. Groundbreaking occurred on May 8, 2023, on a 1,200+ acre site with an investment exceeding $1 billion. Construction progressed rapidly, achieving first production in just 19 months—a timeline described as unprecedented for a facility of this scale.
