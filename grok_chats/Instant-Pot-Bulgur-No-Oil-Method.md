## Assistant

Here’s the easiest, no-oil way to cook perfect bulgur in an Instant Pot (works for both fine and coarse bulgur):

### Basic Ratio & Time (No Oil)
| Bulgur Type       | Water/Broth Ratio | Cooking Time (High Pressure) | Natural Release |
|-------------------|-------------------|------------------------------|-----------------|
| Fine (#1)         | 1 : 1             | 1 minute                     | 10 minutes      |
| Medium (#2)       | 1 : 1.5           | 5 minutes                    | 10 minutes      |
| Coarse (#3 or #4) | 1 : 2             | 7–8 minutes                  | 10–15 minutes   |

### Step-by-Step (Tested Method)
1. Rinse the bulgur well in a fine-mesh strainer (optional but removes dust and excess starch).
2. Add bulgur and water (or broth) directly to the Instant Pot insert. No oil needed.
3. Stir once, lock the lid, set valve to Sealing.
4. Cook on High Pressure for the time in the table above.
5. Let it Natural Pressure Release (NPR) for at least 10 minutes, then quick-release any remaining pressure.
6. Fluff with a fork. It’s ready!

### Tips for Best Texture (Oil-Free)
- If you want it fluffier and less sticky, after cooking spread it on a baking sheet for 5 minutes to let steam escape.
- For tabbouleh (fine bulgur), many people skip pressure cooking entirely and just pour boiling water over it in a bowl (1:1 ratio), cover 20–30 min, then fluff. Works perfectly in the Instant Pot too if you use the 1-minute method.

That’s it — perfectly cooked, oil-free bulgur every time!
