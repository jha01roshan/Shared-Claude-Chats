### Overview of Maneuvers
The Class 7 road test evaluates a range of driving skills to ensure new drivers can handle everyday situations safely. These include basic navigation, parking techniques, and highway integration, all tested in real traffic conditions for about 45 minutes. Preparation involves understanding rules from official manuals, practicing in low-traffic areas, and focusing on smooth control.

**Preparation Tips**  
Start with the ICBC "Learn to Drive Smart" and "Tuning Up for Drivers" guides for step-by-step instructions. Practice each maneuver 10-20 times in empty lots before roads, using commentary to verbalize actions. Always prioritize safety checks like mirrors, signals, and shoulder checks to avoid faults.

**Common Errors to Avoid**  
Forgetting shoulder checks during turns or merges, improper wheel positioning on hills, or rushing parking can lead to failures. Adjust for weather or traffic, and remember: minor errors are okay if overall driving is safe.

### Detailed Guide to Class 7 Road Test Maneuvers
This comprehensive overview draws from official ICBC resources and practical driving tips to provide an in-depth understanding of each maneuver required in the British Columbia Class 7 road test. The Class 7 test, aimed at novice drivers transitioning to full licensing, assesses observation (scanning 12-20 seconds ahead), space management (maintaining 2-3 second following distances), speed control, steering precision, and communication (signals and horn use). Maneuvers are practiced in structured sessions, rated on a 1-3 scale (needs work to competent), and must be executed safely without major errors like ignoring signals or causing hazards. Below, each maneuver is explained with step-by-step procedures, safety considerations, practice advice, and visual aids. For automatic transmissions, focus on smooth braking/acceleration; for manuals, incorporate clutch and gear management without coasting.

#### Intersection Moves
Intersection maneuvers involve navigating turns and straight-through passages while yielding appropriately. Use the "see-think-do" strategy: scan left-center-right-left, assess gaps, and proceed smoothly.

- **Straight Through:** Approach by scanning for signs/lights; if no stop required, maintain speed while checking mirrors. At stop signs/red lights, stop behind the line, leave a car-length gap ahead for maneuverability, keep wheels straight. On green, count to three if following another vehicle, accelerate moderately to match flow.
- **Right Turn:** Signal early, reduce speed, shoulder check right for cyclists, scan intersection, turn hand-over-hand if sharp, straighten while accelerating. Yield to pedestrians/traffic on red after full stop.
- **Left Turn:** Position left in lane, wheels straight while waiting, scan, turn smoothly into left lane without cutting corners. For advance arrows, ensure safe timing; in roundabouts, yield and go counterclockwise.
- **Safety Notes:** Avoid entering blocked intersections; watch for "stale" greens. In multi-lanes, change lanes early.
- **Practice:** Low-traffic areas progressing to moderate; use diagrams to review. Rate on scanning and timing.<grok:render card_id="c6d37d" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Backing Up
Backing requires maximum visibility and slow control to avoid hidden hazards like children or objects.

- **Steps:** Perform 360° check, adjust seat/mirrors, grasp wheel at 12 o'clock with left hand, look over right shoulder through rear window, back slowly straight or as needed, glance forward occasionally.
- **Safety Notes:** Only back when necessary; stop if unsure. Combine with parking/pulling away.
- **Practice:** Empty lots or quiet streets; repeat for straight-line control. Rate on vision and smoothness.<grok:render card_id="5842af" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Entering Traffic
This tests smooth integration from curbs or side streets into flowing traffic.

- **From Curb:** Brake on, shift to drive/first gear, release parking brake, mirror-signal-shoulder check, accelerate smoothly to match speed, cancel signal.
- **From Side Street:** Scan for gaps, yield, accelerate to flow; for acute angles, prepare early.
- **Safety Notes:** Ensure approaching vehicles are at least half a block away; avoid hesitation in merges.
- **Practice:** Moderate traffic; combine with turns. Rate on checks and speed adjustment.<grok:render card_id="a94ca7" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Pulling Over and Stopping at the Roadside
Safe stopping minimizes disruption and hazards.

- **Steps:** Plan spot, mirror-signal-shoulder check right, slow gradually, pull within 30 cm of curb, apply parking brake.
- **Safety Notes:** Avoid blocking driveways; shoulder check before door opening.
- **Practice:** Multi-lane roads; rate on space choice and smoothness.<grok:render card_id="347f4b" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Changing Lanes
Essential for positioning; avoid in restricted areas like crosswalks.

- **Steps:** Check necessity/safety, mirror for gap, signal, shoulder check, steer steadily, center in lane, cancel signal.
- **Safety Notes:** Maintain speed; wait for clear space.
- **Practice:** Straight multi-lanes; rate on checks and position.<grok:render card_id="fe59df" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Parking on a Hill
Prevents rolling; direction depends on curb and slope.

- **Downhill with Curb:** Wheels right, tire to curb, reverse/park gear.
- **Uphill with Curb:** Wheels left, tire to curb, first/park gear.
- **Without Curb:** Wheels right for both.
- **Safety Notes:** Full parking brake always.
- **Practice:** Hilly low-traffic areas; rate on wheel position.<grok:render card_id="66d37d" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Starting on a Hill
Avoids roll-back; use brake or friction point.

- **Uphill Automatic:** Brake to accelerator smoothly.
- **Manual:** Clutch to friction, release brake while accelerating.
- **Downhill:** Straighten wheels if needed.
- **Safety Notes:** Practice both methods.
- **Practice:** Gradual to steep hills; rate on control.<grok:render card_id="14ffd6" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Angle Parking
Diagonal stalls; forward or reverse entry.

- **Forward:** Align bumper with adjacent center, turn sharply, straighten centered.
- **Reverse:** Pass stall, 360° check, back turning wheels, straighten.
- **Safety Notes:** 45 cm margins; easier exit if reversed in.
- **Practice:** Empty lots with cones; rate on judgment.<grok:render card_id="f9f412" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Parallel Parking
Curbside between vehicles; requires precision.

- **Steps:** Align rear with ahead car, back at 45°, straighten when clear, turn left near behind car, adjust forward.
- **Safety Notes:** 360° check; watch swing-out.
- **Practice:** Cones for spaces; rate on position.<grok:render card_id="a24c96" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Stall Parking
Perpendicular stalls; similar to angle but straight-in.

- **Steps:** Mirror-signal-shoulder, turn into stall centered, stop with margins.
- **Reverse:** Back using mirrors/shoulder checks.
- **Safety Notes:** Judge distance accurately.

#### Two- and Three-Point Turns
Reversing direction in tight spaces.

- **Two-Point (Driveway):** Pass driveway, back in turning right, forward out left.
- **Three-Point:** Pull right, turn left across, back right, forward straight.
- **Safety Notes:** Only in safe, low-traffic areas; full checks.
- **Practice:** Quiet roads; rate on steering.<grok:render card_id="5c64a2" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">

#### Merging On and Off a Highway
High-speed integration/exit.

- **On:** Accelerate in ramp to match speed, mirror-signal-shoulder merge.
- **Off:** Signal early, move right, maintain speed until ramp, then slow.
- **Safety Notes:** 3-second space; no stopping in merge lane.

#### General Driving: Straight, on Hills and Curves
Ongoing skills for control.

- **Straight:** Scan ahead, maintain lane/ speed, 2-second distance.
- **Hills:** Adjust speed/brakes, no coasting; watch for visibility loss.
- **Curves:** Slow before, accelerate out, stay in lane.
- **Safety Notes:** Adapt for weather (more space in rain); use ABS correctly.
- **Practice:** Varied conditions; rate overall.<grok:render card_id="9e3523" card_type="image_card" type="render_searched_image">
